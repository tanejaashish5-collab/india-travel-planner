## Quality bar — every campaign must clear all 6

1. Real data only (verifiable against Supabase)
2. Caption-visual coherence (caption dest = visual dest)
3. No on-image text added by us beyond Pomelli's own + monogram bar
4. 5 hashtags max, all niche; banned-list enforced
5. One pillar per campaign — no mixed intent
6. Aspect ratio decided upfront — 4:5 default, 9:16 only for reel covers
