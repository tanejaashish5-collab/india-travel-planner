## The reshape hypothesis (Phase 1/2 must TEST this, not assume it)

**Source unit economics from PUBLIC statutory filings instead of confidential P&Ls.** Franchisee
operating companies and master franchisees in India file financials with MCA/ROC (accessible via
Tofler/Zauba/MCA portal). Deriving and publishing analysis of public filings is standard financial
journalism — no NDA breach (nothing is solicited from franchisees), and truth-on-public-record is
the strongest defamation posture. Test in Phase 1: pick 3 known QSR franchise brands, find ≥5
franchisee-entity filings each via MCA/Tofler, and verify revenue/EBITDA-level data is actually
extractable at unit or entity level. If it is not, the kill memo writes itself. Supplement layers
(legally cleaner than P&Ls): anonymized structured surveys (ranges, not documents), court/consumer-forum
records, commercial real-estate + hiring signals.

## Legal red flags to carry into the red team (kill authority applies)

- **Defamation exposure is live in India**: Edelweiss v. Moody's ($100M suit over a financial-performance
  report) is the direct precedent; BNS §356 is actively used against negative financial reporting.
  Mitigations to red-team: public-record sourcing, methodology transparency, right-of-reply protocol,
  ranges over point claims. The smoke-test plan MUST gate any actual publishing on a one-hour Indian
  media-law consult (paid — founder decision; the factory run itself publishes nothing, so the package
  can be built and red-teamed before that spend).
- **Demand volume is ESTIMATED, not confirmed** — no keyword-volume data was obtainable in the sweep.
  Phase 1 should check search volume for "is <brand> franchise profitable"-class queries (Google
  Trends / keyword tools) and size the inbound thesis honestly.
