# Is Munnar in November worth it?

Short answer: **yes — verdict: go.** NakshIQ's dataset scores November a full 5 out of 5 for Munnar — 10/10, the top tier of its yearly calendar.

## What the score is actually measuring

Munnar's [November page](/en/destination/munnar/november) calls it "the genuine pivot to Munnar high season." The northeast monsoon is spent by mid-month, rainfall drops under 80mm, and the Kanan Devan Hills tea estates — Munnar's defining view — are described as dry and clear for the month. Daytime temperatures run 12–22°C at the destination's 1,600m elevation, firmly in comfortable-trekking-and-sightseeing range.

Eravikulam National Park, the other headline draw, is at full tempo this month. The trade-off the dataset flags: hotel rates climb 20–25% across November as Christmas-week traffic starts booking ahead, so the "quiet before the rush" window is real but closing.

## What it costs

November sits inside Munnar's peak season on our cost ledger (shared with December, January, and February). A mid-range hotel runs a typical **₹4,200 a night** (range ₹2,450–7,200), a homestay around ₹2,950, and a hostel dorm bed about ₹550. Food averages ₹680 a day, a full-day taxi ₹2,500, and intercity transport from the nearest hub (Cochin International Airport or Aluva Railway Station, both roughly 110km away) around ₹1,300. Full breakdown on the [Munnar cost page](/en/cost/munnar).

## Who it actually suits

The dataset's own advice: if you want tea-estate photography without monsoon haze, and you can book before the Christmas-week rate jump lands, November is the cleaner call over December. Ideal stay length and the destination's elevation make this comfortable for most travelers, including families — Eravikulam's entry slots are the one thing worth booking ahead rather than showing up and hoping.

## The one-line answer

NakshIQ's data rates November a clean 10/10 for Munnar — the northeast monsoon is done, the tea estates are at their clearest, and the rates are still a notch below the Christmas peak. Full month-by-month scoring is on the [Munnar destination page](/en/destination/munnar).
