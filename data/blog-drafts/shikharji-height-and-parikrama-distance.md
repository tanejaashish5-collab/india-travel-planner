# Shikharji height and parikrama distance: the real numbers

Short answer: **1,365m** at the summit, and the full parikrama (sacred circuit) covers **27km**, done in a single day, rated **hard** difficulty. This is Shri Sammed Shikharji — Parasnath hill in Jharkhand's Giridih district — one of Jainism's most important pilgrimage sites, and the numbers surprise people who expect a short temple visit rather than a full day on the trail.

## The numbers

Our trek dataset logs the Shikharji Parikrama as a 27km route completed in one day, with a maximum altitude of 1,365m, rated hard. That's not a typo-friendly "easy half-day" climb — it's a genuinely demanding single push, usually started before dawn so pilgrims can complete the full circuit of temples (there are more than 20 along the route) and descend before dark.

Shikharji the destination sits at that same 1,365m elevation — the summit figure and the destination elevation line up, since the hill itself is the pilgrimage site. There's no separate "base camp" elevation in our data to subtract from; the climb essentially starts near the base town and gains most of its height across the full 27km loop rather than in one short steep stretch.

## When to actually attempt it

Our best-months data for Shikharji covers October through March — six months, not a narrow window. That's a wider good-weather season than most high-altitude treks get, because Parasnath hill's 1,365m elevation means it never faces the snow-closure problem that shuts down treks above 3,000m. The constraint here is heat and humidity, not snow: Jharkhand's pre-monsoon and monsoon months (April through September) aren't in the dataset's recommended window, which tracks with a 27km single-day hard route being far tougher in heat and humid air than in cool winter conditions.

## What it costs

Shikharji's peak season (October through March) runs a mid-range hotel at a typical **₹3,150/night** (range ₹1,800-5,400), a homestay around ₹1,850, and a hostel dorm bed at ₹350. Food runs about ₹700 a day, and a full-day taxi is ₹1,650. Outside peak, the same categories drop substantially — a mid-range room falls to roughly ₹1,500. ## Getting there

Shikharji's nearest railhead is Parasnath station, just 15km away — the closer option by far. The nearest airport is Ranchi, about 180km. Most visits run 1-2 days: one day for the parikrama itself, with an optional extra day to rest before or after given the climb's difficulty.

## The one-line answer

1,365m at the summit, 27km for the full parikrama, one hard day — climb it October through March, and start before dawn.
