# Is Darjeeling in February worth it?

Short answer: it works, but it isn't the month to pick if you have a choice. Our ledger scores [Darjeeling in February](/en/destination/darjeeling/february) at **6/10 — wait**.

## What February actually looks like

Temperatures run a cold 3-12°C, with more clear days than January — Tiger Hill sunrise views improve through the month as the winter haze thins. Tea gardens stay dormant, so the plantation-walk part of a Darjeeling trip won't deliver much; this is a month for the Darjeeling Himalayan Railway and the monasteries, not the gardens. If your travel dates are fixed to February, the trip still works on those terms — our ledger notes that Kanchenjunga viewpoint visits and Sundarbans-style wildlife-season extras (Royal Bengal tiger sightings run near their annual high around this time) still land value. What February doesn't do is break the town open the way the main season does.

## The context that matters

Darjeeling's year splits sharply. October and November are the clear peak at **10/10 — go**, March and April follow at 8/10, May sits at 6/10 alongside February — both "wait" months. January is the low point at 4/10 — skip — and June through August drop to the floor at 2/10 during the monsoon, recovering only slightly to 4/10 in September. So February's honest position is: better than the January trough on either side of it, but a full four to eight points below the October-November window on the 10-point scale. If your dates are flexible at all, that's the trade worth making.

## What it costs

Darjeeling's typical per-person daily spend (shared room, two travellers) runs **₹1,400** on a budget trip, **₹4,980** mid-range, and **₹11,790** at the luxury end, for a shoulder-season month — mid-range and luxury travellers typically split a day cab, a backpacker pools a shared jeep four ways. Full seasonal breakdown on the [cost page](/en/cost/darjeeling).

## Getting there

The nearest airport is Bagdogra, about 67km away; the nearest railhead is New Jalpaiguri (NJP), about 74km. Most visits run 3-4 days — enough for the railway, Tiger Hill, and a monastery circuit without rushing.

## Verdict

- **Fixed February dates:** wait, but workable — plan around the railway and monasteries rather than tea-garden walks, and keep expectations realistic on sunrise odds.
- **Flexible dates:** move to October or November if you can; both score a clean 10/10 and deliver the full list, gardens included.
- **January:** actively avoid if you can — it's the ledger's low point for the town.
