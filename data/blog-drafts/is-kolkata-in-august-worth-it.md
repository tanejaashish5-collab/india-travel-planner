# Is Kolkata in August worth it?

Short answer: our ledger scores [Kolkata in August](/en/destination/kolkata/august) at **4/10 — skip**. It's peak monsoon. But Kolkata is not a beach town; rain doesn't switch the city off the way it switches off Goa. So the real question is what you're coming for.

## What August actually looks like

August sits in the heart of the monsoon here. Two things on our ledger close or shrink because of it:

- **The Sundarbans are closed.** If mangroves and river safaris were part of the plan, August removes them entirely.
- **Darjeeling as a side trip becomes weather-dependent.** The hills are reachable only on good-weather days — you can't plan a fixed itinerary around them.

What the rain gives back: the city turns lush and green, and the cultural calendar starts moving again — talks, theatre, the indoor Kolkata of College Street coffee houses and museum halls that never needed sunshine in the first place.

## Who should still come

If your Kolkata is entirely indoors — bookshops, galleries, old-club dining rooms, long afternoons over coffee — August works at a discount in crowds and doesn't ruin the product. Plan 3–5 days, arrive via Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport or Howrah Junction, and accept that every outdoor plan needs a same-day weather check.

If your Kolkata includes river ghats at golden hour, day trips, or the Sundarbans: don't force it.

## The October case

This is the honest part. [Kolkata in October](/en/destination/kolkata/october) scores **10/10 — go** on our ledger, and the note is three words long: Durga Puja — the greatest show. The city rebuilds itself into an open-air art festival, and it is the single best reason to be here all year.

[September](/en/destination/kolkata/september) is the in-between month — 6/10, wait — monsoon retreating while the Puja pandals go up. If your dates flex even slightly, that's the direction to push them.

## Verdict

- **Fixed August dates, indoor-first trip:** come, it's workable.
- **Fixed August dates, outdoor-first trip:** skip — Sundarbans closed, hills unreliable.
- **Flexible dates:** wait for October. It isn't close.
