# Is Chandratal in December worth it?

Short answer: **no — verdict: skip.** NakshIQ's dataset scores December a 1 out of 5 for Chandratal — 2/10, the bottom of its yearly calendar.

## What the dataset actually says

Chandratal's [December page](/en/destination/chandratal/december) is unambiguous: "frozen and inaccessible. −22°C to −5°C. Lake re-frozen. No road, no camps, no visit." At 4,300m, the lake basin that draws summer trekkers is a different place entirely by December — both the Manali-side and Kaza-side approaches are under 4–6 feet of snow, and there are no tent camps running. The dataset's advice is to wait for late June, when the season reopens.

## The closure is already starting, dated

This isn't just an annual pattern — it's already happening. A road update dated **October 15, 2026** reports the Manali–Kaza road's Gramphu–Batal stretch shut by district disaster management after fresh snowfall, with Kunzum La closed to all vehicles the same day. A separate update four days earlier, October 11, 2026, recorded Kunzum La, Baralacha La, and Shinku La all closed after snowfall, with vehicles stranded in Lahaul-Spiti. Treat both as dated snapshots of a fast-moving situation rather than a permanent status, but they confirm the dataset's seasonal call is already playing out on the ground, a full six weeks before December even starts.

## Why there's no cost breakdown here

Most of our destination pages carry a cost section pulled from our rate ledger. We're not including one for Chandratal in December — there's no operating season to price. Tent camps are gone, the road is shut, and quoting a "typical night" rate for a destination with no actual access would be misleading rather than useful.

## What to do instead

If Chandratal is the goal, the dataset's own guidance points to the window between roughly mid-June and early October, before the first snowfall closes Kunzum La for the year. December, January, and most of November are all low-scoring months on the same ledger for the same reason — frozen lake, no road.

## The one-line answer

NakshIQ's data rates December at 2/10 for Chandratal, and a road closure dated October 15, 2026 already confirms the access is gone for the season. Full month-by-month scoring is on the [Chandratal destination page](/en/destination/chandratal).
