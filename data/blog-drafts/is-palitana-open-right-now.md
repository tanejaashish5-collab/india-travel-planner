# Is Palitana open right now, and when does it close for the year?

Short answer: **yes, open right now.** Our verified pilgrimage data lists Shatrunjaya's open months as January through May and October through December — the hill is closed only from June through September, for the Chaturmas monsoon period. That data was last verified June 7, 2026, against Gujarat Tourism's official page plus Wikipedia and Trawell.

## The pattern, not just today's status

This isn't a day-to-day opening hours question — it's a seasonal one. The climb to the 863 Jain temples on Shatrunjaya hill closes every year for roughly four months around the southwest monsoon, then reopens once Chaturmas ends. Our dataset's own note on [Palitana in October](/en/destination/palitana/october) flags the catch: "the exact re-opening date depends on the Jain lunar calendar and varies each year; early October visits risk arriving before the shrines are accessible." By mid-to-late October, that reopening has typically happened — which is why right now, in mid-October, the answer is a clean yes.

## How November, December and January score

All three of the next few months score at the top of our yearly calendar for Palitana:

- **[November](/en/destination/palitana/november):** 5/5 — 10/10. "Perfect climb weather," 16–30°C. The dataset also notes Kartik Poornima falls around November 24, which brings the year's biggest Jain-pilgrim peak — worth booking months ahead if that's your window.
- **[December](/en/destination/palitana/december):** 5/5 — 10/10. "Best — cool, clear summit views," 12–26°C, described as the year's sharpest light over the temples.
- **[January](/en/destination/palitana/january):** 5/5 — 10/10. Called the year's best trek month on our ledger — cool pre-dawn starts for the 3,800-step climb, dry trail, clear peak views.

None of these three months carry a downside note in our data. The destination's own best-months list agrees: November, December, January and February are the four months it's built around.

## What this means for timing a trip

If you're asking "is it open now" because you're worried about a Chaturmas-season closure, you can stop worrying for this stretch — the window stays open straight through winter into May, then closes again around June. The one thing worth checking closer to your travel date, especially if you're coming right at a reopening in future years, is the exact Chaturmas end date for that year, since it moves with the Jain lunar calendar rather than a fixed day.

## The one-line answer

Palitana is open now and will stay open through January at minimum, per data verified June 2026 — the only closure is the recurring June–September monsoon window, not anything happening this winter.
