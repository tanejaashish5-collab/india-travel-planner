# Is Lachung in December worth it?

Short answer: wait if you can, go if your dates are fixed and you accept the risk. NakshIQ's [month-by-month dataset for Lachung](/en/destination/lachung/december) scores December at **6/10 — "wait"**, not a clean go. The reasoning is specific, not vague: very cold (-5 to 10°C), snow, and genuinely dramatic winter landscapes — but the same row flags that roads close frequently mid-month due to snow accumulation, and visitors "can be stranded for days awaiting clearance."

## What the month actually looks like

December sits inside Lachung's broader best-months window, alongside October, November and the March–May stretch — but the dataset still only scores it a "wait", one notch below a clean go. The upside is real: winter clarity at altitude, morning frost reaching all the way down to Bomdila and Tawang, and lower valleys staying serene while the upper passes think about shutting. If your trip is fixed to this month, the dataset's own note says Yumthang Valley will "still get value from it" even without peak conditions.

The downside is the same thing that makes the scenery dramatic: snow accumulation closes roads without much warning, and clearance can take days, not hours. This isn't a maybe — it's the stated reason the score stops short of a go.

## The better alternative, by the same dataset

October scores a clean **8/10 — "go"** for Lachung: roads restored after the monsoon, a lush green valley, and clear skies good for trekking and photography — the dataset calls it "the secondary-best window after April." November sits at 6/10 like December (cold, clear, snow arriving at higher elevations, roads workable early in the month but riskier late). January drops further, to 4/10 — "skip" — with Zero Point "nearly certain to be blocked" and road closures described as frequent and unpredictable.

So the pattern across four consecutive months is: October strong, November and December both a wait, January a skip. December isn't the worst month to go — it's the better half of a weak stretch, not the stretch's strong month.

## What it costs in December

December falls in Lachung's peak-season cost bracket on NakshIQ's rate ledger. A mid-range room runs a typical **₹2,700/night** (range ₹1,550–4,650), a homestay around ₹2,400, and food about ₹840 a day. A full-day taxi — the way most people actually move around this stretch of North Sikkim — runs roughly ₹3,150, and intercity transport from the nearest hub averages ₹2,050. Full breakdown, including the shoulder and low-season numbers, on the [Lachung cost page](/en/cost/lachung).

Lachung sits at 2,600m, reached via Bagdogra airport (250km) or Pakyong (190km), with New Jalpaiguri the nearest railhead at 270km — already a long final stretch before winter road risk enters the picture.

## The one-line answer

If your dates are flexible, move to October. If December is fixed, go in expecting winter scenery worth the trip, but build in slack for a multi-day road closure — the dataset's own "wait" score is built on exactly that risk, not a vague caution.
