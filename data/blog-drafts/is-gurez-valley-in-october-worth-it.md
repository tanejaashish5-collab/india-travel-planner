# Is Gurez Valley in October worth it?

Short answer: our ledger scores [Gurez Valley in October](/en/destination/gurez-valley/october) at **6/10 — wait**. The valley is open, and by most measures October is the cleanest month up here — but it's also the last month, and the door can shut without much warning.

## Why October is different from the rest of the season

Gurez only exists as a tourist destination for part of the year. It sits at 2,400m in Bandipora district, reached from Srinagar (123km) over the Razdan Pass at roughly 3,300m — a road that's only open June through October. Everything about visiting Gurez is downstream of that fact.

By October, the valley is rapidly cooling: 0–12°C, with the pass liable to close on any given day once the first proper snowfall arrives. Autumn colour along the Kishanganga is brief but real, and the crowds that fill June through September have mostly gone home. That's the case for coming. The case against it is simpler — the trip only works if the pass stays open, and nobody controls that.

Compare it to what came before: [September](/en/destination/gurez-valley/september) scores a clean **10/10 — go**, with "best clarity" weather (5–20°C), golden meadows and sharp views of Habba Khatoon peak. [August](/en/destination/gurez-valley/august) sits at **8/10 — go**, pleasant and still fully staffed for camping. October is the only month on the calendar where the destination itself might not be reachable by the time you arrive.

## What to actually plan for

Gurez has no luxury stays — budget days run around ₹1,700, midrange near ₹4,600, on top of an ₹800+ transport line for the rough Bandipora road and any permit logistics. There's no infrastructure to fall back on if the pass closes early; you're either through it or you're not. [Full cost breakdown here](/en/cost/gurez-valley).

If you're coming in October, check pass status the day before you leave Srinagar, not the week before. Build a buffer day into the itinerary rather than a tight loop, and don't book onward travel that assumes a same-day return over Razdan.

## Verdict

- **Fixed October dates:** go, but check the pass daily and carry a buffer day — this is genuinely the last chance of the year.
- **Flexible dates:** push to September if you can. It's the same valley without the closure risk.
- **Risk-averse trip, kids or elderly in the group:** wait for next June.
