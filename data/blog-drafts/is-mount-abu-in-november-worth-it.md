# Is Mount Abu in November worth it?

Short answer: yes. Our ledger scores [Mount Abu in November](/en/destination/mount-abu/november) at **10/10 — go**. It sits inside the town's longest run of good months, not as an isolated exception.

## What November actually looks like

Temperatures run a cool, clear 12-25°C — comfortable for every outdoor stop on the usual list, from Nakki Lake to the Dilwara Temples to a Guru Shikhar sunrise. November also lines up with the peak of Gujarat's tourist season: Mount Abu sits just across the border and is the closest hill escape for Ahmedabad and Vadodara, so expect genuine weekend crowds. Booking ahead for Friday-Saturday nights isn't optional advice here, it's the difference between a room and a full house.

## The context that matters

November isn't a lucky outlier — it's the middle of a five-month run. October, November, December, January and February all score a clean **10/10** on our ledger; March holds at 8/10 before April drops to 6/10 as the heat ramps up. May and June are the genuine low point at **4/10 — skip**: unusually hot for a hill station at 28-38°C, with minimal relief over the plains below. July and August sit at 6/10 — the monsoon turns the Aravallis green and the waterfalls run, but it's a "wait," not a highlight. September recovers to 8/10 as the rain eases and crowds start returning.

So the honest framing: if your dates are flexible, October through February are all genuinely strong, and November doesn't need to carry the trip alone — it's one good week in a long good season.

## What it costs

Mount Abu's peak-season pricing (October through March minus April) runs a mid-range hotel at a typical **₹6,100/night** (range ₹3,500-10,500), a homestay around ₹3,500, and food at about ₹1,300 a day. A full-day taxi runs ₹4,800. Off-peak (May-August), the same categories drop by roughly half. Full breakdown on the [cost page](/en/cost/mount-abu).

## Getting there

Mount Abu has no airport or railhead of its own — the nearest airport is Udaipur (160km) and the nearest railhead is Abu Road (28km), the last stretch by road. Most visits run 1-2 days, which is enough for the standard circuit of Nakki Lake, Dilwara, and Guru Shikhar.

## Verdict

- **Fixed November dates:** go — one of five consecutive 10/10 months.
- **Flexible dates:** any of October through February works about as well; pick based on crowds (quieter in January) versus festival energy (busier around Gujarat's holiday weekends in November-December).
- **May–June:** skip. The heat genuinely erodes what makes a hill station worth visiting.
