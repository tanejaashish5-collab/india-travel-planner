# Shatrunjaya hill climb distance: how far is it from Palitana?

Short answer: **7km round trip** (3.5km each way), climbed in a single day to **603m**, rated moderate difficulty on our trek dataset. Our destination notes consistently put the step count at roughly **3,800 steps** to reach the 863 Jain temples at the summit — a demanding half-day-plus climb, not a multi-day trek.

## The numbers

Our trek dataset logs the Shatrunjaya climb at Palitana as a 7km route, 1-day duration, moderate difficulty, topping out at 603m. Our separate pilgrimage-route data records the one-way distance as 3.5km, which doubles to the 7km round trip the trek entry carries. Palitana itself sits at 469m elevation, which puts the summit about **134m** above the town — most of that gain happens on the stepped ascent rather than at the base.

Our destination notes describe the walk as "the 3,800-step climb" across several months of data, not as a single offhand mention. One month's note rounds it differently, to "3500-step" — we're flagging that discrepancy rather than silently picking one figure. Treat 3,800 as the number our data uses consistently, with that single outlier on record.

## When to actually climb it

Palitana's month-by-month data is unusually direct about the climb's seasonal window. November and December both score a clean **10/10 — go** ("perfect climb weather" and "best — cool, clear summit views" respectively), and January holds at **10/10** too ("cool pre-dawn starts... dry trail, clear peak views — the year's best trek month"). February eases to 8/10, and March drops to 6/10 as heat starts building on the exposed steps.

April and May fall hard, to **4/10** and **2/10** — the dataset is blunt about why: by April, heat-stroke risk is "extreme on exposed basalt sections," and by May, surface temperatures on the steps can exceed 55°C by 11am, effectively closing the climb to tourist use. June through August hold at 2/10 for a different reason: the climb closes mid-June for Chaturmas, the Jain monsoon religious retreat, and the 863 temples stay shut to pilgrim flow through mid-September. October only recovers to **6/10 — wait**, because the exact Chaturmas reopening date shifts with the Jain lunar calendar each year, and an early-October visit risks arriving before the shrines have reopened.

## What it costs

Palitana's peak season (November through February) runs a mid-range hotel at a typical **₹2,950/night** (range ₹1,700-5,100), a homestay around ₹1,750, and a hostel dorm bed at ₹350. Food runs about ₹750 a day, and a full-day taxi is ₹1,800. The low season (April through September, when the climb is either closed or genuinely dangerous) drops those same categories by roughly a fifth. Full breakdown on the [cost page](/en/cost/palitana).

## Getting there

Palitana has no airport or railhead of its own — the nearest of either is Bhavnagar, 50km away. Most visits run 1-2 days, enough for the climb itself plus a buffer for the pre-dawn start our data recommends.

## The one-line answer

7km round trip, roughly 3,800 steps, one day — climb it November through February, and avoid mid-June through mid-September, when it's both dangerously hot in the shoulder weeks and formally closed for Chaturmas in between.
