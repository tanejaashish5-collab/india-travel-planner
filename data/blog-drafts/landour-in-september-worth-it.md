# Landour in September — the post-monsoon reveal

If you're asking about Landour weather in September, here's the ledger answer first: [Landour in September](/en/destination/landour/september) scores **10/10 — go**. It's one of the two best months of the year on this ridge, and the reasons are specific.

## What September does to the weather

September is when the monsoon recedes from Landour and the spectacular autumn transition begins. The practical effect, from our ledger: the air is rinsed clean by three months of rain, the long Himalayan views are at their most photographically alive, and everything on the hill — cafés, walks, homestays — is fully running rather than half-shuttered the way it can be in the wet months.

At **2,270 m**, Landour sits a couple of hundred metres above Mussoorie, which is exactly why the post-monsoon clarity lands harder here: the viewpoints at Lal Tibba and the walks around the loop face straight out at the ranges the rain has just unveiled.

## Who September at Landour suits

Our data flags this month as built for the slow trip — the writers'-retreat pattern of long mornings at Char Dukan and afternoons on empty walking loops. It also rates **8/10 for families**. This is not an activity destination in any month; September just makes the core product (quiet, views, walks) as good as it gets.

Plan **2–3 days**. The railhead is Dehradun (35 km); the airport is Dehradun Jolly Grant (60 km).

## The Mussoorie comparison

Worth being honest about the neighbour one ridge down. [Mussoorie in September](/en/destination/mussoorie/september) scores **8/10** — the monsoon's slow retreat — against Landour's 10. By [October](/en/destination/mussoorie/october) both hit 10/10 as the season fully opens. If your trip is Mall Road, Kempty Falls and the classic circuit, Mussoorie is the base; if the point is silence and long views, September belongs to Landour.

## Verdict

Go. September and [October](/en/destination/landour/october) are the window this place is built around — book for it, don't drift into it. Month-by-month scoring is on the [Landour destination page](/en/destination/landour).
