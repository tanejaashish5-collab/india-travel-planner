# Is Adi Kailash in December worth it?

Short answer: our ledger scores [Adi Kailash in December](/en/destination/adi-kailash/december) at **0/10 — skip**. Adi Kailash sits at 5,945m near the Kumaon border with Tibet, and December isn't a "bring a warmer jacket" month there — it's a month the route itself stops being usable.

## Why December fails here, specifically

Our data is blunt about the mechanism: winter is fully established by December, with all passes above 4,500m closed by snow. Temperatures fall to -15 to -20°C, and the road toward the border turns genuinely hazardous rather than just uncomfortable. The Inner Line Permit office — which every visitor needs to clear before heading toward the border belt — operates on minimal capacity through the month, which alone can stall a trip before the weather even becomes the problem.

Altitude sickness and acclimatisation risk compound all of this. At close to 6,000m, December's cold isn't a logistics inconvenience layered on top of a normal trek — it's the difference between a route that's passable and one that genuinely isn't, with limited ability to turn back quickly once committed.

## What a trip here actually needs

Adi Kailash is accessed via Pantnagar (nearest airport) or Tanakpur (nearest railhead), both a long approach drive before the high-altitude stretch even begins. Our ledger sets an ideal visit length of 5-6 days — this is not a weekend add-on to a Kumaon trip, it's a dedicated expedition-style journey, and that structure matters more in a month where the final stretch may simply not be open.

A [cost reference for Adi Kailash](/en/cost/adi-kailash) exists on our ledger, but it's built around the months the route is actually viable — not December, when most of what that budget covers (permit processing, the final approach) isn't functioning as normal.

## When to actually go

Our data flags April, May, June, September and October as the viable window, explicitly listing November through March as avoid months. September and October are the stronger pick of that set: the monsoon has cleared, the route is at its most reliably open, and visibility toward the peak is at its best before winter begins closing passes again. April through June works too, ahead of the monsoon, for travelers who'd rather catch the thaw than the autumn light.

## Verdict

- **Determined to go this December:** don't. This is a closed-route month, not a tough-but-doable one — our data flags it unsafe, not just unpleasant.
- **Flexible dates, serious about the trip:** shift to September or October — same destination, an actually-open route, and the clearest stretch of weather on the calendar.
- **Building a Kumaon itinerary around winter:** keep Adi Kailash out of it entirely this season and swap in a lower-altitude stop instead.
