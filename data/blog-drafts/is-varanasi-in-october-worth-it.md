# Is Varanasi in October worth it?

Yes. **Verdict: go.** October is the opening month of NakshIQ's longest peak window for Varanasi — the dataset scores it a clean 5 out of 5, 10/10 on the site's displayed scale, and holds that score all the way through March.

## What changes in October

Varanasi's [month-by-month page](/en/destination/varanasi/october) logs the shift plainly: temperatures ease to 22–32°C, the post-monsoon skies clear, and the ghats — swollen and partly submerged through the rains — re-emerge fully walkable. The dataset ties October to the start of the region's festival calendar: Dussehra and the run-up to Diwali fall inside the month, and Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat is logged as running at its most elaborate. It's the version of Varanasi that shows up in most cultural photography and documentary work — the one the site says the month is "built for."

By contrast, the preceding low season (May through August) scores lower across the same dataset: the Ganga runs high, heat sits in the mid-30s to 40s, and several ghats stay partially underwater. October is the clean handoff out of that window, not a marginal improvement on it.

## What it costs

Hotels step up to Varanasi's peak-season pricing in October, alongside the five months that follow it. A mid-range double runs a typical ₹5,200 a night (range ₹3,000–9,000), up from ₹2,350 in the May–August low season. A homestay typically comes in around ₹3,000, and a hostel dorm bed runs about ₹700. Food averages ₹1,100 a day per person. A day taxi is the steepest jump — ₹4,100 versus ₹1,850 off-peak — since ghat-to-ghat routing gets busier as the pilgrim and tourist calendar fills. Full breakdown, category by category, on the [Varanasi cost page](/en/cost/varanasi).

## Who it suits, and the trade-off

The dataset marks October as manageable with older kids (6/10) rather than a natural family pick — the ghats, the crowd density, and the early-morning boat rituals reward travelers who can walk long stretches and sit through slow, unstructured mornings. Varanasi's ideal stay runs 2–4 days, enough to work the ghats at both dawn and dusk without rushing.

The catch is booking pressure, the same one that shows up in most first-of-peak months: October opens the six-month window that carries Varanasi through winter, so rooms near the ghats fill early and last-minute rates run above the typical figures above. Varanasi Junction and Lal Bahadur Shastri airport both handle the month's traffic without issue — the constraint is accommodation, not access.

## The one-line answer

NakshIQ's data doesn't hedge on this one: October is a top-scoring month for Varanasi, not a shoulder-season compromise — book the ghat-facing room early, since the peak window has just opened and everyone chasing the same clear skies is looking at the same six months.
