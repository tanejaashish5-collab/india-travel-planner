# Is Mount Abu in December worth it?

Short answer: yes. Our ledger scores [Mount Abu in December](/en/destination/mount-abu/december) at **10/10 — go**. It's peak winter at Rajasthan's only hill station, and the Christmas-New Year rush from Gujarat is the one thing worth planning around.

## What December actually looks like

Temperatures run a cold, clear 8-22°C — cool enough for a shawl at Nakki Lake in the evening, comfortable for every daytime stop on the circuit. Sunrise at Guru Shikhar, Rajasthan's highest point at 1,722m, is the dataset's specific callout for this month. The catch is timing: the Christmas and New Year window pulls heavy traffic from Ahmedabad and Vadodara, both a short drive away, and our note is direct about it — book accommodation at least two weeks ahead if your dates fall anywhere near the holidays.

## The context that matters

December isn't an isolated good month — it's the middle of a long run. October through February all score a clean **10/10** on our ledger, five consecutive months. March holds at 8/10 before April drops to 6/10 as the heat builds. May and June are the real low point at **4/10 — skip**: 28-38°C is unusually hot for a hill station, with little relief even at 1,220m elevation. July and August sit at 6/10 — monsoon greens the Aravallis and the waterfalls run, but it reads as a "wait," not a reason to go. September recovers to 8/10 as the rain eases.

So if your dates are flexible: any of October through February works about as well as December. The trade-off inside that window is crowds (heaviest around Christmas-New Year and Diwali-adjacent weekends) versus quiet (January, once the holiday rush clears).

## What it costs

Mount Abu's peak-season pricing — which covers December — runs a mid-range hotel at a typical **₹2,700/night** (range ₹1,550-4,650), a homestay around ₹2,950, and a hostel dorm bed at ₹410. Food runs about ₹900 a day per person, and a full-day taxi is ₹2,100. Outside the Christmas-New Year peak, the same categories can run noticeably lower even within December's own window — worth asking before booking. Full breakdown on the [cost page](/en/cost/mount-abu).

## Getting there

Mount Abu has no airport or railhead of its own — the nearest airport is Udaipur (160km) and the nearest railhead is Abu Road (28km), the last stretch by road. Most visits run 1-2 days, enough for Nakki Lake, the Dilwara Temples, and a Guru Shikhar sunrise.

## Verdict

- **Fixed December dates:** go — peak of a five-month good run, but book 2+ weeks ahead around Christmas and New Year.
- **Flexible dates:** January is the quieter equivalent once the holiday crowds clear; November and October trade a few degrees of warmth for lighter weekend traffic.
- **May–June:** skip. The heat genuinely erodes what a hill station is for.
