# Is Mahabaleshwar in June worth it?

Short answer: no. Our ledger scores [Mahabaleshwar in June](/en/destination/mahabaleshwar/june) at **2/10 — skip**. The monsoon arrives by the second week, bringing 700-1,000mm of rainfall that closes the hill station's famous viewpoints on landslide risk and puts real danger into the ghat roads up from Pune and Mumbai.

## What June actually looks like

Temperatures sit at a comfortable-sounding 18-25°C at 1,353m, but comfort isn't the issue — access is. Pratapgad Fort becomes inaccessible once the rain sets in, and the Sahyadri viewpoints that Mahabaleshwar is built around close for the same reason the roads do. This is the start of what our ledger treats as a three-month closure: June, July and August all score at the bottom of the scale (1-2/10), with July the worst of the three for outright shutdown.

The one thing June does have going for it is the drama — waterfalls in spate, valleys turned fully green — but that's a trade a traveler makes deliberately, not a default recommendation.

## Where the ledger disagrees with the calendar

There's no genuine upside case for June here the way there sometimes is for a "skip" month elsewhere. May, the month before, is also rated skip (2/10) for pre-monsoon heat and haze. September, the month after, only turns to "wait" (3/10) — and even then, our data flags the legitimate trip as the last week of the month only, once the viewpoints start clearing and Pratapgad reopens around September 25.

## The months that actually work

October changes everything: **10/10 — go**, and it holds through November and December as the strawberry season ramps up at the Mapro Garden farms and the Sahyadri views open up to 50-60km visibility. November and December score the same 10/10. If dates are flexible at all, that's the three-month window to build around instead.

## Getting there and the numbers

Nearest airport is Pune (PNQ), about 120km away; the nearest railhead is Wathar, roughly 60km out. Plan 2-3 days. Our ledger puts a budget day near ₹1,700, mid-range near ₹4,200, and a fuller trip near ₹9,900 — see the [Mahabaleshwar cost page](/en/cost/mahabaleshwar) for the full breakdown, which also notes that hotel rates run cheaper mid-week even in season.

## Verdict

- **June:** skip — this is month one of a three-month monsoon closure, not a quieter alternative to the crowds.
- **September:** wait, and only worth it in the last week once the roads and viewpoints reopen.
- **October through December instead:** go — full strawberry season, clear Sahyadri views, and the year's best version of the hill station.
