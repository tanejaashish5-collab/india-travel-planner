# Is Puducherry in December worth it?

Short answer: yes. Our ledger scores [Puducherry in December](/en/destination/puducherry/december) at **10/10 — go**, the best month of the year alongside a strong run either side of it.

## What December actually looks like

Daytime temperatures sit in the 22-29°C range as the northeast monsoon retreats through the first half of the month — rainfall roughly halves compared to November. That's the trade: November still carries the tail of the monsoon, December is where the skies properly clear while the coast stays cool enough to walk the promenade and the French Quarter by day. The catch is the calendar, not the weather — Christmas and New Year week drives a 50% rate premium and books out the French Quarter's heritage hotels 21-30 days ahead. If your dates are flexible, the first half of December gets the same weather without the holiday surge.

## The context that matters

December isn't a lucky one-off — it sits inside Puducherry's longest good run. October through March all score **go**, with December, January and February at the ledger's top mark (10/10), November close behind at 8/10, and October and March a step down at 6/10. May and June are the low point at 4/10 — "wait," not an outright skip, but the pre-monsoon heat and humidity make it the weakest stretch. The honest framing: if your dates are flexible, any week from mid-November to February works about as well as December — pick based on whether you want the Christmas-New Year energy or a quieter version of the same town.

## What it costs

Puducherry's typical per-person daily spend (shared room, two travellers) runs **₹1,450** on a budget trip, **₹4,740** mid-range, and **₹12,370** at the luxury end — mid-range and luxury travellers usually split a day cab between them, a backpacker pools a shared auto or cab four ways. December sits inside the peak-season window, so expect the upper end of that range, especially around Christmas-New Year. Full seasonal breakdown on the [cost page](/en/cost/puducherry).

## Getting there

Puducherry has its own railway station, and the nearest airport is Chennai, roughly 150km and a 3-hour drive away. Most visits run 2-4 days — enough for the French Quarter, Auroville, and a couple of beach afternoons without rushing.

## Verdict

- **Fixed December dates:** go — one of the three best months of the year, though book heritage-hotel rooms well ahead if your trip overlaps Christmas-New Year.
- **Flexible dates:** mid-November through February all land in the same strong window; the first half of December is the sweet spot between weather and crowd.
- **May-June:** the weakest months — not a skip, but not the one to pick if you have a choice.
