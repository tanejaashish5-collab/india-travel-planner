# Is Sissu open in January?

Short answer: the town itself usually stays open in some form, but reliably getting there and staying comfortably is a different question. NakshIQ's own January dataset scores Sissu **4/10 — skip**, and a road update dated **November 14, 2026** — more than six weeks before January even begins — already showed the route beyond Koksar closed by snow, with only the Atal Tunnel-to-Sissu-to-Koksar stretch itself still open.

## What our dataset says about the month

Sissu's [month-by-month page](/en/destination/sissu/january) rates January a skip, and the note is specific: "the high passes are shut, fuel stations closed past the last town, most guesthouses unheated — only the committed winter traveler should try." The skip reason given is just as direct — heavy snowfall and sub-zero temperatures make roads treacherous and many guesthouses closed. Sissu's best-months window, independently, is May, June, September and October; January doesn't appear in it, and neither do December or February, both of which score the same 4/10 skip for the same underlying reasons.

## What the road looked like on November 14, 2026

A road update dated November 14, 2026 reports that the Atal Tunnel (Rohtang Tunnel) itself was open, and Sissu and Koksar were accessible through it — but onward routes beyond Koksar were already closed. That's weeks before December even starts, let alone January, and it lines up with a string of other Lahaul-area closures logged the same week: the Manali-Keylong highway was shut on November 13 after 2-3 feet of snowfall at Dhundi, and by November 15, Himachal Pradesh's PWD was counting 16 closed roads across the Lahaul valley.

Treat that as current to its own date, not a permanent reading — road status on this corridor shifts through the winter as BRO clears and re-closes sections, and we don't have anything more recent than mid-November. The pattern it shows, though, is consistent with exactly what our January dataset is warning about, just arriving earlier in the season than the calendar month itself.

## What this means for a January trip

- **The dataset's seasonal call:** skip, independent of any single day's road status — January sits inside Sissu's three-month skip bracket (December, January, February) on access and comfort grounds together.
- **The most recent road signal:** as of November 14, 2026, the Atal Tunnel approach was open as far as Sissu and Koksar, but routes beyond Koksar were already shut — before winter had properly set in.
- **If your dates are fixed:** don't assume the tunnel bypassing Rohtang Pass means the whole corridor is safe — snowfall at either portal, or beyond Koksar, can still close the roads Sissu itself depends on.

## What it costs, if you go anyway

January falls in Sissu's lowest-cost bracket on our rate ledger — a mid-range double runs a typical **₹3,350/night** (range ₹1,950-5,650), a homestay around ₹1,250, and a hostel dorm bed at roughly ₹430. Food runs about ₹700 a day, and a full-day taxi is ₹2,750. Full breakdown on the [Sissu cost page](/en/cost/sissu).

## The one-line answer

Sissu in January is a dataset "skip" backed by road closures that were already showing up a month early — if your trip isn't fixed to this month, May-June or September-October is the window both signals agree on.
