# Jaipur, Jodhpur, Udaipur and Ranthambore in mid-November — weather, crowds, what to expect

This is a question that comes up often from people planning a Rajasthan circuit in November: is the weather good, and how crowded does it get? Our ledger doesn't track week-by-week splits — only full-month scoring — but the month-level answer is unusually consistent across all four stops: **every one of them scores 10/10 — go.**

## What November actually looks like, stop by stop

**Jaipur** — 12–28°C, listed as "excellent" on our ledger: cool, clear air, a full festival calendar, and peak tourist season beginning. The Pushkar Camel Fair runs late October into November, and Diwali light displays are still up around the city, including at Nahargarh. Heritage hotels book up early this month, per our note — worth acting on ahead of the trip rather than closer to the date.

**Jodhpur** — also 12–28°C and also at peak season. Mehrangarh's zip-line and museum are flagged as being at their best this month, and the Umaid Bhawan gardens are described as lush. Same heritage-hotel booking pressure applies here too.

**Udaipur** — a touch milder at night, 14–28°C, with our note calling out "festive season" and the Lake Palace at its best for evening views; rooftop restaurants are specifically flagged as working well in this weather.

**Ranthambore** — 14–28°C and, notably, this is listed as peak safari season with the best tiger-sighting odds of the year: dry forest, animals concentrated at waterholes. The booking note here is firmer than the others — safaris are flagged as needing roughly 60 days' lead time, which matters if a Rajasthan trip is being planned less than two months out.

## What "peak season" means for a trip plan

All four scoring a clean 10/10 in the same month is not the norm across our ledger — it usually means one or two stops compromise for the sake of the itinerary. November is the exception: this is simply the best month, calendar-wide, to do this exact circuit. The flip side is booking pressure. Heritage hotels in Jaipur and Jodhpur, and Ranthambore safari slots specifically, are the three things worth locking in well ahead rather than on arrival.

Our data here is month-level, not week-level — so if the real question is "early November vs. late November," the honest answer is that the ledger doesn't distinguish between them. What it does confirm is that the whole month sits inside each destination's best-months window, so there's no particular reason to prefer one half of November over the other based on our scoring — the difference, if any, would come down to specific festival dates or safari slot availability, not a weather shift.

## Verdict

- **Planning this circuit for November:** go — all four stops are at or near their best this month, and the weather works for everything from Jaipur's forts to a Ranthambore game drive.
- **Booking less than 60 days out:** Ranthambore safaris are the tightest constraint; sort that first, then build hotels in Jaipur, Jodhpur and Udaipur around it.
- **Flexible on exact dates within November:** our month-level data gives no reason to pick one week over another — go by festival dates and safari availability instead.
