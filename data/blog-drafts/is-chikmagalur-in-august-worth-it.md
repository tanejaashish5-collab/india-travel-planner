# Is Chikmagalur in August worth it?

Short answer: our ledger scores [Chikmagalur in August](/en/destination/chikmagalur/august) at **4/10 — wait**. The southwest monsoon is still running — 16–25°C with 600–800mm of rain — and it holds the same pattern as July rather than breaking from it.

## What's open and what isn't

NH-73, the road most visitors rely on, is stabilising rather than reliably clear — our data notes closures still run 1–2 days a week. The Mullayanagiri summit road stays shut most days through the month. What does work: Hebbe Falls reaches peak flow and is visible from the base even without the summit access, and the plantation canopy is at its most saturated green, offering natural shelter from the rain if you're moving between coffee estates rather than trying to summit anything.

The ledger is direct about who this suits: monsoon waterfall photographers and adventurous trekkers comfortable with mud get real value from August. Families, casual hikers, and anyone uncomfortable with landslide-prone roads are flagged to wait.

## Why September is the better bet if your dates are flexible

One month out, the picture changes meaningfully. [September](/en/destination/chikmagalur/september) scores **8/10 — go**: the monsoon retreats, rainfall eases to 300–500mm, and Mullayanagiri reopens. [October](/en/destination/chikmagalur/october) holds at **10/10 — go**, the full season opening at 14–26°C with plantations still at their greenest from the residual northeast monsoon. Both months keep the lush, just-rained look that draws people to Chikmagalur in the first place, minus the road closures.

## Planning around it

Chikmagalur sits at 1,090m in the Coorg & Chikmagalur region of Karnataka, reached via Mangalore Airport (152km) or Bangalore Airport (250km), with Kadur railway station the closer railhead at 40km. The ideal stay is 2–3 days.

Plantation homestays with meals are the standout value here, typically ₹2,000–4,000 a night. Budget days on the whole run near ₹1,500, midrange around ₹4,600, luxury up to ₹10,600. [Full cost breakdown here](/en/cost/chikmagalur).

## Verdict

- **Flexible dates:** wait for September or October — same green landscape, none of the road-closure risk, and the summit actually opens.
- **Fixed August dates, comfortable with mud and rain:** go for the waterfalls specifically, but don't plan around reaching Mullayanagiri's summit.
- **Traveling with kids, or on a tight timeline that can't absorb a closed road day:** wait.
