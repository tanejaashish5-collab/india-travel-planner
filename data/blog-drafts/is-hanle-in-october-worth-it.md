# Is Hanle in October worth it?

Not really. **Verdict: skip.** NakshIQ's dataset scores October only 2 out of 5 — 4/10 — for Hanle, its lowest tier. The reason is straightforward: October sits outside the destination's core June–September season, and the numbers reflect what that gap actually costs a traveler.

## What the score is measuring

Hanle sits at 4,500m in eastern Ladakh, home to one of the highest-altitude astronomical observatories in the world and a dark-sky reserve that draws stargazers specifically. The [month-by-month page](/en/destination/hanle/october) logs October as rapidly cooling — temperatures from -5°C to 10°C — with roads starting to ice over and the Umling La pass liable to close without warning. It's still technically the tail of the stargazing season, but the dataset is explicit that conditions have already turned harder and local services have scaled back from their summer footing.

Compare that to September, which scores a full 10/10 in the same dataset: 0–15°C, the driest and clearest skies of the year, the Milky Way arch at its most dramatic, and Umling La still reliably open. September is the last month the site rates as a clean "go" before the shoulder-season trade-offs of October set in.

## What it costs

October falls into Hanle's shoulder-season pricing bracket, alongside May. A mid-range hotel runs a typical ₹7,700 a night (range ₹4,400–13,000) — cheaper than the ₹11,000 peak-season rate, which is one upside of traveling outside the window if the conditions don't put you off. A homestay runs about ₹4,400, food averages ₹1,600 a day, and the mandatory permit fee is ₹900. A day taxi — necessary given there's no railhead and the nearest airport is Leh, 250km away — runs roughly ₹6,500. Full breakdown on the [Hanle cost page](/en/cost/hanle).

## The honest trade-off

If the trip is fixed to October for reasons outside the weather — a specific astronomy event, a broader Ladakh circuit already in motion — the dataset's read is that this isn't the version of Hanle that makes the destination worth the detour. Roads need checking daily before departing Leh, and the stargazing window this late in the season is a diminishing one, not the peak experience the destination is known for.

## The one-line answer

NakshIQ's data is clear that October is the wrong month for Hanle on its own merits — if the dates can move, September is the same destination at 10/10 instead of 4/10, for a genuinely different trip.
