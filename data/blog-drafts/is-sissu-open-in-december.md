# Is Sissu open in December?

Short answer: not reliably, and as of the most recent road update, the approach itself was closed. NakshIQ's own December dataset scores Sissu **4/10 — skip**, and a road status update dated **November 13, 2026** reports the Manali-Keylong highway and the Atal Tunnel's Manali-side approach shut by 2-3 feet of snowfall. Those are two separate signals pointing the same way.

## What our dataset says about the month

Sissu's [month-by-month page](/en/destination/sissu/december) rates December a skip, and the note is direct: passes shut, most guesthouses closed, services minimal, and attempting the month without winter-specific plans is "asking for a rescue." Our data recommends targeting Sissu for May-June or September-October instead, when the Atal Tunnel corridor is reliably open. Sissu's own best-months window, independently, is May, June, September and October — December doesn't appear in it.

## What the road looked like on November 13, 2026

A road update dated November 13, 2026 reports that the Deputy Commissioner of Lahaul-Spiti closed the Manali-Keylong highway (NH-3) and the Atal Tunnel approach for all vehicle categories, after 2-3 feet of snowfall at Dhundi (the tunnel's south portal) and in the Lahaul valley (the north portal, where Sissu sits). The Kullu-Manali stretch was restored by that same morning, but the Keylong-side approach — the side Sissu is on — remained closed, with BRO running snow-clearance operations on the Udaipur-Tandi-Keylong road from both ends of the tunnel.

Treat that as current to its own date rather than a permanent condition — road status on this corridor changes through the winter as BRO clears and re-closes sections, and we don't have a reading more recent than November 13. If you're planning a trip, checking the live status immediately before you leave Manali is not optional on this route in winter.

## What this means for a December trip

- **The dataset's seasonal call:** skip, independent of any single day's road status — December sits outside Sissu's recommended window on cost, crowd and access grounds together.
- **The most recent road signal:** closed, as of November 13, 2026, specifically on the Manali-side approach through the Atal Tunnel.
- **If your dates are fixed:** don't assume the tunnel itself solves winter access — the tunnel bypasses the Rohtang Pass closure, but snowfall at either portal can still shut the approach roads on either side.

## What it costs, if you go anyway

December falls in Sissu's lowest-cost bracket on our rate ledger — a mid-range double runs a typical **₹3,350/night** (range ₹1,950-5,650), a homestay around ₹1,250, and a hostel dorm bed at roughly ₹430. Food runs about ₹700 a day, and a full-day taxi is ₹2,750. Full breakdown on the [Sissu cost page](/en/cost/sissu).

## The one-line answer

Sissu in December is a dataset "skip" backed by a dated, specific road closure — if your trip isn't fixed to this month, May-June or September-October is the window both signals agree on.
