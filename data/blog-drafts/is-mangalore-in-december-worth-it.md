# Is Mangalore in December worth it?

Short answer: yes. Our ledger scores [Mangalore in December](/en/destination/mangalore/december) at **10/10 — go**. It's peak season on the coast — dry, 22-31°C — and the Mangalorean Catholic community's Christmas celebrations are at their grandest, though hotel rates run double for it.

## What December actually looks like

The month sits firmly in dry, bathable-sea conditions, with 22-31°C and no monsoon interference. The dataset's specific callout is Christmas: Mangalorean Catholic Christmas celebrations run through the city this month, and hotel rates climb to 2x their usual rate around the Christmas-New Year window — plan bookings well ahead if your dates land anywhere near it.

## The context that matters

December sits in the middle of a long good run on our ledger. November through February all score highly — November and December both 10/10, January and February also 10/10 — before March eases to 8/10 as humidity starts climbing. April and May drop to 6/10 and 4/10 as pre-monsoon heat and humidity build, and June through August bottom out at **2/10 — skip**: 900-1200mm of rainfall, beaches closed, sea bathing prohibited. September stays weak at 4/10 as the sea remains rough through mid-month, and October recovers to 8/10 once the season properly opens.

So if your dates are flexible, any month from November through February performs about as well as December — the trade-off inside that window is Christmas-NYE crowding and 2x rates versus the quieter, same-weather alternative of January.

## What it costs

Mangalore's peak season — which covers December — runs a mid-range hotel at a typical **₹2,300/night** (range ₹1,350-3,900), a homestay around ₹1,750, and a hostel dorm bed at ₹410. Food runs about ₹800 a day, and a full-day taxi is ₹2,200. The Christmas-New Year week itself runs well above even this peak bracket, per the dataset's 2x note — worth asking ahead before booking that specific week. Full breakdown on the [cost page](/en/cost/mangalore).

## Getting there

Mangalore has its own airport (Mangalore International, IXE) and its own railhead (Mangalore Junction / Mangalore Central) — no onward transfer needed. Most visits run 1-2 days, easy to combine with Udupi or Karkala as side trips.

## Verdict

- **Fixed December dates:** go — peak weather, but budget extra and book ahead specifically for the Christmas-New Year week.
- **Flexible dates:** January matches December's weather almost exactly, without the holiday surcharge.
- **Avoid June-August:** monsoon rainfall of 900-1200mm shuts down sea bathing and collapses heritage walks.
