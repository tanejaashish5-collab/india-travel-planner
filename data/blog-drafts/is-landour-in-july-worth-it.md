# Is Landour in July worth it?

Short answer: not really. Our ledger scores [Landour in July](/en/destination/landour/july) at **4/10 — skip**. The problem isn't the cantonment itself — it's the approach. The monsoon breaks hard on the outer Himalayan crest this month, and landslide risk on the roads up from Dehradun is real even on days when Landour is sitting dry above the cloud line.

## What July actually looks like

This is the quirk of Landour's geography: it sits at 2,270m on a ridge that can stay clear while the lower approach roads take the brunt of the rain. That's a real phenomenon, not a marketing line — but it doesn't help a traveler who's stuck on a landslide-blocked road below. Our ledger rates June, the month before, only slightly better at "wait" (3/10), for the same reason: it's the peak-access month right up until the monsoon actually arrives, usually by the second week.

August holds at the same 4/10 skip rating as July — the outer ranges are deep in monsoon, and the same road-condition caveat applies to the whole belt, not just the direct route in.

## Where the ledger is clearer

There's no legitimate upside case for July the way there sometimes is for an off-season month. This isn't a quieter, cheaper version of Landour's good months — it's a genuinely different risk profile, tied to road conditions rather than the destination's own weather.

## The months that actually work

September is where Landour turns, and turns decisively: our ledger scores it **10/10 — go**, as the monsoon recedes and the post-monsoon reveal begins — rinsed air, the mountains at their most photographically alive, and operators still fully running. October holds the same 10/10, called out as arguably the cleanest month of the year up here. April and May also score well (10/10 and 8/10), for the spring window before the rains arrive. We've written up [Landour in September](/en/blog/landour-in-september-worth-it) in more detail if that's the month you're actually planning around.

## Getting there and the numbers

Nearest airport is Dehradun's Jolly Grant, about 60km away; the nearest railhead is Dehradun itself, 35km out. Plan 2-3 days. July falls in Landour's low season on price, too — our ledger puts a mid-range hotel night near ₹2,700 and a day's food near ₹550, both well under the peak-season rate. That's a real saving, just one that comes with the access risk above attached.

## Verdict

- **July:** skip — the destination can be fine; the road getting there often isn't.
- **June and August:** the same caveat applies, at a similar rating.
- **September or October instead:** go, for the clearest, most reliable version of Landour all year.
