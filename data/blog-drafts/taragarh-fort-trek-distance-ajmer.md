# Taragarh Fort trek distance: how far is it from Ajmer Dargah?

Short answer: **6km**, done in a single day, reaching **870m** at the fort — rated moderate difficulty on our dataset. It's a half-day climb above the city, not the multi-day trek some searches seem to expect.

## The numbers

Our trek dataset logs Taragarh Fort as a 6km route with a 1-day duration and a moderate difficulty rating, topping out at 870m. Ajmer itself sits at 486m elevation, which puts the fort roughly 384m above the city — most of that gain comes in the second half of the climb, once the trail leaves the built-up area near the Dargah.

The walk starts close to Dargah Sharif, which is why most searches frame it as "Dargah to Taragarh" rather than naming a separate trailhead — there isn't a meaningfully different starting point to account for.

## When to actually climb it

Ajmer's own month-by-month data makes the seasonal call straightforward. October through March score **4-5/5 (8-10/10)**: temperatures sit in a manageable 8-32°C range across those months, with November and December both hitting the clean 10/10 ("ideal... dry, clear skies" and "cool... crisp mornings"). February is specifically called out as "perfect walking weather for the Taragarh Fort hike."

April through August drop to **1-3/5 (2-6/10)** — the dataset is blunt about why: by April, the dargah courtyard marble "burns feet by noon" and there's "limited shade on the Taragarh trek," with a recommendation to carry at least 3 litres of water if you go anyway. May and June are worse still, with midday temperatures reaching 43°C and outdoor exploration flagged as dangerous. August adds a different problem — monsoon rain leaves "muddy trails to Taragarh Fort." September sits at a middling 3/5 (6/10) as the heat eases but humidity lingers.

## What it costs

Ajmer's peak season (October, November, December, February, March) runs a mid-range hotel at a typical **₹6,100/night** (range ₹3,500-10,500), food at about ₹1,300 a day, and a full-day taxi at ₹4,800. The shoulder months (January, April, September) drop to roughly ₹4,200/night mid-range; the low months (May-August) fall further to around ₹2,700/night. ## Getting there

Ajmer's nearest airport is Jaipur (135km); the city has its own railhead at Ajmer Junction. Most trips run 1-2 days — enough to combine the Taragarh climb with the Dargah Sharif and Ana Sagar Lake, or extend into a Pushkar day trip.

## The one-line answer

6km, one day, 870m at the summit — climb it October through March, and specifically avoid the midday window if you're there April through August.
