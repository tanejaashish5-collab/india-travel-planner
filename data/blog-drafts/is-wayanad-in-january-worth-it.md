# Is Wayanad in January worth it?

Short answer: yes, without qualification. NakshIQ's [month-by-month dataset for Wayanad](/en/destination/wayanad/january) scores January at a clean **10/10 — "go"**, describing it as the "peak Wayanad window." The temperature range runs 12-28°C across the destination's 700-2100m elevation spread, and the dataset notes Edakkal Caves, Chembra Peak, Banasura Sagar and the Wildlife Sanctuary are all running at full operational tempo.

## What the month actually looks like

January isn't an edge case inside Wayanad's season — it's the middle of it. December scores the same 10/10, described as "peak season" with Christmas-New Year rates running 2-2.5x normal, Chembra Peak slots getting tight, and Wildlife Sanctuary bookings needing a 5-7 day lead. February also holds at 10/10, called the destination's driest month, with coffee flowering becoming visible. March drops only slightly to 8/10, the last clearly cool month before the heat picks up.

That's four consecutive strong months, with January sitting squarely in the middle — past the Christmas-New Year rate spike and booking crunch that December carries, but with the same weather and the same full-tempo sights.

## Why that matters for booking

If the New Year surcharge and tight slots that December's dataset note flags are a concern, January gets you the same 10/10 conditions without that specific pressure — the dataset doesn't record a similar rate-spike or booking-lead warning for January itself. Chembra Peak's trek permits and Wildlife Sanctuary safari slots are still worth booking ahead regardless, since both run on fixed daily capacity, but the acute New Year crunch is a December-specific note, not a January one.

## What it costs in January

January falls in Wayanad's peak-season cost bracket on NakshIQ's rate ledger. A mid-range room runs a typical **₹3,400/night** (range ₹2,000-5,850), a homestay around ₹3,200, and food about ₹850 a day. A full-day taxi runs roughly ₹2,500, and intercity transport from the nearest hub averages ₹1,250. Full month-by-month breakdown, including the shoulder and low-season rates, on the [Wayanad cost page](/en/cost/wayanad).

Wayanad sits at 780m, reached via Calicut International Airport (95km) or Kozhikode railway station (95km) — both the same distance out, which keeps the final approach simple whichever you pick. The dataset's ideal stay is 3-4 days, enough to cover the caves, the peak trek and a safari without rushing any one of them.

## The one-line answer

January is Wayanad at its best by every measure the dataset tracks — if you're choosing between December, January and February, January gets you the same 10/10 conditions as December without the New Year rate spike.
