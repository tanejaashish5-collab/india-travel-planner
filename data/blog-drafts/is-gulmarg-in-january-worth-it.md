# Is Gulmarg in January worth it?

Short answer: it depends what you came for. Our ledger marks [Gulmarg in January](/en/destination/gulmarg/january) as **go** for skiing, but the underlying comfort score is low — 2/10. Both numbers are honest; they're measuring different things.

## What January actually looks like

Temperatures run -8 to 2°C — deep winter, with passes often locked and the valley floor the quietest version of the place you'll see all year. That's exactly the setup peak ski season needs: reliable powder snow, Gondola Phase 1 and 2 both operational, and Apharwat peak accessible for anyone past the beginner slopes. The note on our ledger calls January built for winter-snow first-timers specifically — this is not a month for sightseeing or sunny valley walks, it's a month for skiing, and the "go" verdict is scoped to that purpose. Ski packages are worth booking two weeks ahead; January is Gulmarg's busiest month for exactly the reason that makes it worth visiting.

## The context that matters

The low comfort score isn't a one-off dip — it's the trade-off of deep winter at 2,650m. December and June score highest on our ledger at 10/10, with September also at 10/10; May, October and the shoulder months of April, July and August sit in the "wait" range (6-8/10); January, February and March all carry the same low 2/10 comfort score as January despite being "go" for skiing — the cold and limited road access are the constant, not a January-specific problem. The honest framing: if you're coming for skiing, January through March are interchangeable and all legitimate; if you're coming for a comfortable valley visit with easier road access, June or September line up better on both scores.

## What it costs

Gulmarg's typical per-person daily spend (shared room, two travellers) runs **₹1,470** on a budget trip, **₹5,230** mid-range, and **₹14,590** at the luxury end — this is shoulder-season pricing; January's peak-ski-season rates run higher, especially for ski-in stays near the gondola base. Full seasonal breakdown on the [cost page](/en/cost/gulmarg).

## Getting there

Gulmarg's nearest airport is Srinagar, about 56km away; the nearest railhead is Jammu Tawi, considerably further. Most ski trips run 2-3 days, which covers a couple of full days on the slopes plus travel.

## Verdict

- **Coming to ski:** go — January, along with February and March, is the core of the season. Book gondola passes and gear ahead; this is peak demand.
- **Coming for a comfortable valley visit, not skiing:** wait — June and September score far better on comfort, with easier access and a wider temperature range.
- **Flexible on purpose:** if snow is the point, January delivers it reliably; if comfort is the point, it doesn't.
