## 404 intentional (scraper garbage) — 3 URLs

| URL | What it is | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| `/$` | Scraper bot writing literal `$` into URL — never a real path | "Validate Fix" — they stay 404, but they age out as Google stops re-checking. Optional: **Removals tool → Temporarily hide URL** for faster eviction. |
| `/&` | Same — literal `&` from a scraper | same |
| `/10` | Numeric-only URL from a bot guessing pagination | same |

**Do NOT add redirects for these.** Redirecting `/$` → `/` would create a small but real surface for scraper abuse (and Google could later flag the chain). The intentional 404 is the correct response.

## Recommended action (one click, done in 3 min)

1. Open GSC → **Pages** → "Why pages aren't indexed" → click **Not found (404)**
2. Top-right → click **VALIDATE FIX**
3. Wait 24-72h for Google's recrawl batch. The 25 non-garbage URLs will pass.
4. **If validation still reads Failed after 72h on just the 3 garbage URLs**: open **Removals → Temporary removal → New request** for each of `/$`, `/&`, `/10` (or all under one prefix if URL-encoding allows). 6-month hide is fine; they don't return organic value.

That's it. No commits, no deploys, no middleware edits.

## Why the validation got stuck

Most likely chain of events:
- **Apr 14-20**: a sub-route added/restored (the `region/{state}/{month}` route looks like it landed around then — 7 of 28 URLs are this exact pattern, all locale-mixed). During the rollout window, those URLs briefly served 404.
- GSC crawled during the window and cached "Not found (404)" for these 28 URLs.
- Subsequent commits brought all routes back to 200 + added the 5 redirects (skip-list, region-to-state, kasol-parvati-vs). The 322-URL Apr 20 validation kicked off → cleared 294 URLs progressively as it re-crawled → stalled on the last 28 (likely crawl-budget exhaustion before the validation batch's window closed).
- The "Failed" status reflects that the validation **ran out of recheck attempts**, not that the URLs are actually broken.

**Going forward:** after any route/middleware change, click "Validate Fix" on any open buckets within 7 days rather than waiting for Google's own cadence. The `_triage-gsc-404s.mjs` script will auto-classify any future bucket in 30 seconds.
