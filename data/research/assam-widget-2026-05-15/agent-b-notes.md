## 1. SIVASAGAR — Research

**Gems anchored:**
- **Talatal Ghar / Kareng Ghar**: ASI Monument of National Importance. 7-storey palace (4 above + 3 below). Foundation by Suklenmung 1540 (wooden) → brick rebuild by Rajeswar Singha 1752 ("present permanent structure"). Two tunnels: 3 km to Dikhow River, 16 km to Garhgaon. Mortar = Bora chaul (sticky rice) + egg + lime. Sources: Govt of Assam Sivasagar tourism portal (sivasagar.assam.gov.in/tourist-place-detail/277), Wikipedia Talatal Ghar entry.
- **Rang Ghar**: ASI = "Colosseum of the East". First built by Rudra Singha in bamboo-and-wood, rebuilt by **Pramatta Singha 1744-1751** (NOT 1746 as brief stated — the brief''s "1746" is mid-range; actual range is 1744-51). Roof = inverted Ahom long-boat; stone crocodiles on top. 3 km from Sivasagar centre at Rupahi Pathar. Watched buffalo fights during Rongali Bihu. Sources: Wikipedia Rang Ghar, Incredible India sivasagar/rang-ghar, Assam Tribune.
- **Joysagar Tank + Namdang Stone Bridge**: Joysagar = 318 acres, built 1697 in 45 days by Rudra Singha for mother Joymoti. Wikipedia and multiple sources call it "among the largest hand-dug tanks in India" (some say largest in Asia). Namdang Bridge = 1703, single 60-metre stone slab, 6 culverts, still carries NH-37 traffic. Mortar same Bora-chaul-egg-lime. Sources: Wikipedia Joysagar Tank, Wikipedia Namdang Stone Bridge, Assam Info.

**Eats:** Existing DB = Maichang + Heritage Jaysagar (already covers anchors). Brief''s suggestions Chap-Chap and Hotel Bhardwaj returned poor footprint. Picked **Gadadhar Restaurant** (Babupatty) — appears across Holidify, Swiggy, Travel Setu as a Sivasagar Assamese-thali fixture. Confidence is moderate (not legendary, no founding-year anchor); flagged is_legendary=false, established_year NULL.

**Stays:** Hotel Shiva Palace (value) confirmed on Booking.com + Tripadvisor + MakeMyTrip with mixed-but-current reviews — clean rooms / dated bathrooms, ₹2,000-2,500 band. Hotel Brindavan (xfactor) confirmed on MakeMyTrip and Tourmyindia mention — picked over fabrications-risk options like "Tocklai Tea Bungalow" (Tocklai is in Jorhat district, not Sivasagar — cross-dest exclusion).

**Fabrications rejected:**
- "Itakhuli Inn" (brief suggestion) — zero web footprint. Listicle ghost. Rejected.
- "Heritage Bungalow Sivasagar" (brief suggestion) — no specific property by this name in Sivasagar. Generic listicle template. Rejected.

## 2. CHARAIDEO — Research

**Gems anchored:**
- **Charaideo Moidams**: UNESCO inscription **26 July 2024** at 46th WHC session in New Delhi (some sources say 28 July — Britannica and PIB lock 26 July). India''s 43rd WHS, 1st cultural inscription from NE India. 90 surviving moidams; 30 ASI-protected. CM Himanta Biswa Sarma unveiled the plaque **5 February 2026**. Source: UNESCO whc.unesco.org/en/list/1711, PIB Release 2037495, New Kerala, The News Mill (Feb 2026).
- **Sukaphaa''s Moidam**: **Sukaphaa died 1268 and was buried at Charaideo**. Brief had "Sukaphaa''s moidam (founder ~1268)" — verified correct. Ahom dynasty founded 1253 at Charaideo (the dynasty start, NOT the burial). Brief stated dynasty 1228-1826 (598 years); Wikipedia confirms Ahom kingdom 1228-1826. Source: Wikipedia Charaideo, Britannica Moidams, Vivekananda International Foundation paper.
- **Lachit Borphukan birthplace**: Born **24 November 1622** at Charaideo. Father = Momai Tamuli Borbarua. Battle of Saraighat = **1671** (some sources say March 1671). Lachit Maidam (burial) is in Hoolungapara near Jorhat 15-16 km from Jorhat station — that''s the JORHAT site, not Charaideo. Birthplace = Charaideo. Sources: Wikipedia Lachit Borphukan, Simplified UPSC, Drishti IAS, Incredible India Jorhat Lachit-Borphukan-Maidam page.

## 3. MAJULI — Research

**Gems anchored:**
- **Samuguri Satra Mukha Shilpa**: Hem Chandra Goswami = Padma Shri 2023 (NOT 2022). Conferred by President of India in Civil Investiture Ceremony April 2023 (PIB Press Release 1909724). Operates mask-making school since 1984. Brief stated "National Award" — actually Padma Shri is the right anchor. Sources: PIB, Wikipedia Hem Chandra Goswami (artist), Sentinel Assam, India Today NE.
- **Auniati Satra**: Founded 1653 by Sri Sri Niranjana Deva Goswami under Ahom king Jayadhwaj Singha''s patronage. Akash Bonti tradition (Kati Bihu / mid-October) continuous since 1653. Brief''s "1653 Niranjan Pathakdeva" — the name in primary sources is "Niranjana Deva Goswami" (Niranjana Pathakdeva is alt spelling); fact verified. Sources: auniati.org official, Wikipedia, Holidify, NativePlanet.

Sources: PARI/Rural India Online dedicated feature "A Mising feast at Risong''s Kitchen", The Weekend Leader feature, Tripadvisor reviews on La Maison parent listing.

## 4. JORHAT — Research

**Gems anchored:**
- **Tocklai Tea Research Institute**: 1911 (Indian Tea Association established the Tocklai Experimental Station). World''s oldest and largest tea research centre, continuous operation 114 years. Source: tocklai.org official, Wikipedia Tocklai Experimental Station, Govt of Assam Jorhat district page. Brief stated "1911 (Robert Cuthbert and Sir Edward Gait)" — those names not verified in available sources; safer wording attributes to ITA only.
- **Dhekiakhowa Bornamghar**: Eternal lamp continuously burning since 1461 — lit by Madhabdev (NOT Sankardev personally as brief implied; Sankardev is Madhabdev''s guru, but the lighting attributed to Madhabdev). India Book of Records listing. 13 bighas, 3.5 km off NH-37, 15 km from Jorhat. Sources: Wikipedia, Incredible India, Jorhat District portal, jorhat.gov.in.
- **Cinnamora Tea Estate**: Maniram Dewan established it. Date is contested across sources — primary anchors: 1850 (most common, Assam Info, Wikipedia Maniram Dewan); 1845 (some sources); 1843-44 (Chenimore variant). Stuck with 1850 — strongest consensus across primary sources. Maniram hanged 1858 for 1857 revolt support. Brief stated "1837 some sources" — that date NOT supported by current research; downgraded the brief''s "1837" claim. Sources: Wikipedia Maniram Dewan, The Better India, Assam Info.

**Eats anchored:**

Cuisine = Italian / Pan-Asian / Continental (NOT Assamese — adds variety to DB which already has Chouka + Meridian as the Assamese anchors). Source: Tripadvisor d25788352, Restaurant Guru, Zomato, Justdial.
