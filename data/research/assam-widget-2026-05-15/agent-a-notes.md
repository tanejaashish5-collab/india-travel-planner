## Factual corrections to the brief

1. **Jatinga "documented since 1905 by E.P. Gee"** — WRONG. E.P. Gee was a British tea planter who documented the phenomenon in his 1957 book *Wild Life of India*, visiting with ornithologist Salim Ali in the late 1950s. Brief's 1905 date is unsourceable; the first written record is 1957. CORRECTED in gem prose.

2. **Umananda Guinness 1990s claim** — UNVERIFIED. Brief said "smallest inhabited river island per Guinness 1990s claim — DROP if not verifiable from 2 indep sources today". No Guinness page surfaces. The Govt of Assam Tourism, Incredible India, and Wikipedia all use "smallest inhabited river island in the world" without Guinness citation. DROPPED Guinness; kept the Govt-of-Assam + Incredible-India anchor.

3. **Pobitora "highest rhino density"** — brief mentioned as Guwahati gem candidate. Wikipedia + Govt of Assam confirm "106 rhinos in 38.8 sq km — highest density in India". Distance varies in sources from 30km to 60km; consensus ~40km. I did NOT include Pobitora because it's too well-known to qualify as a hidden gem (it's a destination in its own right in NakshIQ taxonomy).
