## 🚨 Issues Found

**1. 🔴 CRITICAL — Autoposter outage since 2026-05-22 07:58 UTC (~29 h).**
The `autoposter-state` branch has been frozen at `72691a3e` for ~29 hours. The
May 22 evening post, the May 22 analytics sync, and every May 23 slot through
21:47 AEST have produced no run. The autoposter is effectively **down**.

**2. 🟠 Leading hypothesis — the 2026-05-22 Supabase free-tier egress freeze.**
Every autoposter run begins with a Supabase content sync (`── Syncing Nakshiq
content ──` → `Synced → 20 destinations … total catalog=505`). The two runs that
*did* complete (07:02 and 07:57 UTC May 22) synced fine — so Supabase was
healthy until ~08:00 UTC May 22. After that, the documented egress freeze hit:
CLAUDE.md records that the **2026-05-22 Hindi DB re-pass blew the 5.5 GB
free-tier egress cap and the Supabase API 402'd for hours**, and `origin/main`
shows the recovery work on May 23 morning (`1ee1fb0c` "re-trigger Vercel deploy
— bust ISR caches stale from Supabase freeze", `9fc805ea` "egress guardrails
after 2026-05-23 free-tier freeze", `0423226c` "Hindi DB re-pass translation
pipeline"). A 402 on the sync step would crash autoposter runs **before** the
state-push step → no commit → invisible to this audit. The outage onset lines up
exactly with the freeze. *This is inference — it cannot be confirmed without the
Actions logs, because crashed runs leave no trace.*

**3. 🟠 Unexplained — no runs on May 23 even after Supabase recovered.**
Supabase recovery work landed ~01:00–04:00 UTC May 23 (Pro upgrade + guardrails).
If the freeze were the *only* cause, the May 23 03:17 / 04:17 / 11:47 UTC crons
should have synced cleanly and pushed commits. They didn't. Candidate causes
that need the Actions tab to resolve: (a) GitHub silently dropping scheduled
crons in low-traffic UTC hours — a failure mode the watchdog YAML *explicitly
documents* ("GHA's documented unreliability of scheduled crons"); (b) the
**watchdog itself not firing**, or its `AUTOPOSTER_PAT` secret expired so it
can't dispatch catch-ups (the watchdog comments note it "silently dropped on
2026-05-15"); (c) runs still crashing post-recovery for another reason. The
watchdog runs 10×/day specifically to backstop exactly this — and it has
produced no recovery either, which is itself a red flag.

**4. 🟡 SKILL.md audit task file is stale — flagged for the 4th straight audit.**
It still describes the pre-May-16 schedule and its Step 7 re-trigger modes
(`tourist-map`, `canva-visual`, `reel`, `infographic`) all target **paused**
slots — and it has **no mode for the morning/evening feed post or analytics
sync**, which are the slots that actually broke today. The file should be
rewritten to the 6 active crons + explicit paused list, with re-trigger modes
that match.
