THE STORY RULES (new in v2):
1. LINE 1 IS THE COVER and it OPENS A QUESTION. 2. HOLD THE ANSWER. NakshIQ and its verdict arrive no earlier than line 6 and after at least 35% of the words; aim for the middle. The viewer must want the answer before it comes.
3. EVERY LINE BEFORE THE ANSWER RAISES THE PRICE OF BEING WRONG. Use a cost (leave approved, money paid), a clock (the cart, the deadline), a disagreement between the two of them, or a near-miss. A line that only restates the plan is cut.
4. USE THE TWO PEOPLE. They want different things, or one doubts, or they bet. Keep it specific and small (whose pick, who carries the bags, who has to admit it).
5. THE TURN COSTS SOMETHING. When the answer lands, someone loses the argument, a booking moves, a bet is paid. Not a frictionless swap.
6. THE PAYOFF IS AN OUTCOME, said in plain words: what they did, who was right, what happened. Not a mood. No chai, coffee or "quiet morning" ending.
7. NakshIQ enters as the ANSWER to the question line 1 opened, said plainly (name the place, the month, the verdict). Never "Then I checked NakshIQ" (retired), never a pitch.
