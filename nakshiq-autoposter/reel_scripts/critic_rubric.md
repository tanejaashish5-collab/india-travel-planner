# NakshIQ reel script critic rubric (BRIEF v3, 2026-10-01 evening)

The critic is a separate, fresh-context reviewer. It sees the script, the data facts and this rubric, never the writer's reasoning or drafts. script_gate.py already enforces the mechanical rules (7-11 lines of 9-24 words, joining words, no fragments, line 1 = cover.hook, 6 shots, every fact matches the destination's fact pack, no dashes, no undeclared numbers). This rubric covers judgement.

Score each criterion 0, 1 or 2. Quote the line(s) that justify every score below 2.

| # | Criterion | 2 = | 0 = |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Question at line 1.** A stranger reading only line 1 (the cover) wants to know something specific. | a concrete open question (who was right, what they almost did) | a plan or a mood; nothing to find out |
| R2 | **Escalation.** Every line before the answer raises the price of being wrong (cost, clock, conflict, near-miss). | 3+ distinct escalations, no restating | lines restate the plan |
| R3 | **The answer is earned.** NakshIQ arrives as the answer to the question line 1 opened, when the viewer wants it. | product = the answer, mid-script | product is a pitch or arrives with no question pending |
| R4 | **The people matter.** The cast the angle names (parents, friends, a child, a sister) and their difference (want, doubt, worry) drive the tension. | the conflict IS the engine | they are scenery, or the cast is the default couple |
| R5 | **Payoff is an outcome.** The end says what happened or who was right. | concrete outcome in plain words | a mood, or "and it was right" with nothing shown |
| R6 | **Ordinary stakes.** No melodrama (honeymoon, anniversary, birthday, proposal, parent's promise, illness, injury, death); no fear without resolution. | small, believable, specific | any melodrama |
| R7 | **Flow.** Reads aloud as one person telling a friend what happened: every line a full sentence that leads into the next, no fragment stacks ("December came. Filter coffee by the window."), no caption-sounding lines. | continuous, natural, a joining word where it helps | choppy, over-chopped, or written-sounding |
| R8 | **Claims only the facts.** No invented reason, weather, road, price, crowd or event; verdict said exactly as the data has it. | nothing beyond the facts | any invented claim |
| R9 | **Hindi.** Everyday spoken Devanagari a person would say, not a translation. | natural | stiff or literal |
| R10 | **Shootable.** 6 shots, the angle's cast, 1-2 locations, no crowds or text on screen, no deity or sanctum, no real business interior, the shots cover the beats. | yes | needs things the format can't show |

**Pass:** total at least 15 of 20, AND no 0 on R1, R3, R5, R6 or R8.

Return per script: the ten scores, the total, PASS/FAIL, the single weakest line (quoted) and up to 3 numbered fixes. Do not rewrite the script.
