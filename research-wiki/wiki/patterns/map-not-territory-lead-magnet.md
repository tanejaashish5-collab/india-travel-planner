# Map, not territory (what a lead magnet actually withholds)

**The pattern:** a working free artefact gives away the **complete map** — the full shape of the
system, every step named, nothing hidden about *what* the process is — and withholds the
**territory**, the operative detail of *how* each step is executed. The reader finishes knowing a
system exists, believing it works, and knowing they don't have it. **That gap is the product.**

The common failure is the opposite in both directions: give away nothing (no proof of competence, no
reason to trust) or give away a thin slice of everything (useful to no one, proves nothing).

## The reference implementation ([[src-sansoni-funnel-teardown]], 2026-08-06)

A 29-minute free "masterclass" walks all **15** steps of a named, trademarked method — deal
psychology, deal search, first approach, deal overview, first meet, deal analysis, deal strategies,
bundling, deal terms, deal discovery, deal rework, deal close, structure, empire integration. Every
single one resolves to *"I'll show you how"* / *"I'll spoon-feed you the process"*.

Nothing is faked. The map is real, it is complete, and it is genuinely useful — you leave knowing
more than you arrived with. What you cannot do is execute a single step.

**Supporting moves in the same artefact:**
- **Name the sale in the first 90 seconds and frame it as qualification** — *"chat with my team and
  see if you qualify"*. Inverts the posture: the viewer spends the rest hoping to be selected.
- **Objections spoken in the viewer's own voice** before they can raise them ("Will it work? Will it
  work *for me*?").
- **External urgency** — a real, dated, checkable demographic trend rather than a countdown timer.
  Far more durable, and it survives the viewer going away to verify it. (His numbers were 2–3×
  inflated, but the *form* of the move is sound — see [[receipts-over-content]].)
- **Named proof with specific figures** — "Keoni, naturopath, bought a meals business, $0 down" —
  never "a client of mine".

## The honest version of the same structure

The mechanic does not require withholding in bad faith. **What makes it ethical is whether the
territory is actually available at a fair price, and whether the map alone leaves the reader better
off.** His map does leave you better off; his contract is what doesn't (see
[[src-aaron-sansoni-verdict]] — the anger was never about the free training).

For us the inversion is stronger still: if the artefact *is* the product and there's no high-ticket
back-end behind it, we can give the territory too. The lead magnet still works, because the value
was never the withholding — it was **making the system legible in one artefact**.

## Our application

Do **not** gate what ranks — 918 organic sessions/week and gating destination pages is SEO
self-harm. Adapted rule: **don't gate the pages, build one thing worth an email that is not a page.**
His VSL is ungated; his method is the gate. Our destination pages are already the map for *one*
decision; we have never shipped the artefact that makes the decision *system* legible across all of
them.
