# Passion-fit gate (HARD gate, founder-set)

**The rule:** passion-fit + existing-domain-knowledge is a **non-negotiable hard gate** on any idea recommendation. Born 2026-06-16 after Ashish rejected two fully-"validated" ideas (AU-super, AU-edu): *"don't gel with passion."* Validation without passion-fit is wasted research.

## The four lanes

1. **India wisdom & philosophy** (Chanakya, Vedic psychology, Sadhguru-OS)
2. **Travel & experiencing India** (NakshIQ)
3. **Self-mastery & inner growth**
4. **Business & wealth education**

Plus: AI stays the production engine, never the product ("AI-forced is NEGATIVE" — good business first, AI as execution edge). Content is **fully faceless** (AI voice + stock/AI visuals, zero Ashish on camera).
