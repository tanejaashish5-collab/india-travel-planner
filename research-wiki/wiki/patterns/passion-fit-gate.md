# Passion-fit gate (HARD gate, founder-set)

**The rule:** passion-fit + existing-domain-knowledge is a **non-negotiable hard gate** on any idea recommendation. Born 2026-06-16 after Ashish rejected two fully-"validated" ideas (AU-super, AU-edu): *"don't gel with passion."* Validation without passion-fit is wasted research.

## The four lanes

1. **India wisdom & philosophy** (Chanakya, Vedic psychology, Sadhguru-OS)
2. **Travel & experiencing India** (NakshIQ)
3. **Self-mastery & inner growth**
4. **Business & wealth education**

Plus: AI stays the production engine, never the product ("AI-forced is NEGATIVE" — good business first, AI as execution edge). Content is **fully faceless** (AI voice + stock/AI visuals, zero Ashish on camera).

## The FranchiseLens fix, confirmed working (2026-07-12)

FranchiseLens (2026-07-09) got the order wrong: it validated first, found a real gap, THEN the founder rejected it on fit — wasted the full validation spend. The 2026-07-12 PURSUE refill ran the gate FIRST, before any validation: 18 candidates → 8 fit-gate kills/duplicates → 8 reached refute-mode validation, and every one of the 8 was genuinely in Ashish's lanes (no AlgoVerify-style "validated but he'd shrug" survivor reached the expensive phase). **The fix works** — but it has a limit: fit-first ordering saves validation cost, it cannot manufacture a market gap where none exists. All 8 fit-gate survivors still came back CROWDED on competition grounds; see [[rejected-ideas]] and [[src-pursue-refill-2026-07-12]].

## Challenged (and reconciled) 2026-07-27

[[src-alok-sama-money-trap]] — Alok Sama calls "follow your passion" **"terrible advice, for two reasons"**: most people don't have a passion, so the advice just makes them feel inadequate; and even with one there's no playbook ("can I apply somewhere to be Raj Shamani?"). His ordering: **make a living first — *"money buys you things, but the most valuable thing money can buy you is your freedom"*** — then indulge the passion (he wrote his book at 58, after a second master's, once the money was secured).

**Reconciliation — the gate stands, unchanged.** This gate is not "chase what excites you"; it is a filter against *validated-but-unsustainable* recommendations, where Ashish lacks domain knowledge and would abandon the work (the AU-super/AU-edu rejections). Alok is attacking passion as a **career-selection strategy for income**, which we don't do either: [[src-money-plan-2026-07-22]] already makes the unglamorous contract-BA funnel Track 1, the rent-payer, with the passion lanes running off the proceeds. Both say the same thing — **secure the freedom first, then spend it on the lanes.** Log it as external corroboration of the money plan's ordering, not as pressure on the gate.

## How it composes with the other gates

Founder-fit and demand are nearly uncorrelated (2026-06-08 scout: highest-demand idea scored fit 2/5) — which is exactly why the gate exists.
