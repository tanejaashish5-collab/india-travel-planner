# Strategist checklist (the 35-item idea screen)

Consolidates what great operators actually demand — Masters' Union founders (Screwvala, Fixderma, MobiKwik, Zepto, Shazam…), Tony Robbins, [[basesh-gala]], Bhavin Shah; distilled from 1000+ founder patterns. Runs automatically in the radar pipeline (`strategist-checklist.mjs`, 55 self-tests); failing 2+ load-bearing items caps an idea at PARK.

## Structure

- **Idea-quality screen (14 items)** — real paying demand proven BEFORE build (explicit payments or 50+ customer conversations — not enthusiasm); >50% gross margin; a moat competitors can't copy in 12 months (proprietary tech / regulation / network effects / data / owned distribution); never compete on price.
- **Execution playbook (14 items)** — outcome is ~99% execution / 1% idea; ship a real product within ~1 week; speed compounds defensibility; own a distribution channel with compounding free acquisition (40%+ growth must not depend on paid spend); bootstrappable — survives a 50% revenue cliff without raising.
- **Founder-fit (7 items)** — reads [[passion-fit-gate]] / FOUNDER-PROFILE as fixed source; hire attitude over credentials; system runs without the founder.

## Key aphorisms it encodes

- **"Money is truth serum"** — validate willingness-to-pay before building → [[test-cheap-before-build]].
- **Moat over novelty** — moat is the universal weak spot across our scouts (2026-06-08: 0 GREEN on moat, ~2-3/5 everywhere).
- Dual-lens scoring: cash-flow shapes scored with venture-only items excluded, so profitable non-VC businesses aren't unfairly capped.

Composes with: [[tata-gate]] (final veto after scoring), [[ledger-overview]] (tier assignments come from this).
