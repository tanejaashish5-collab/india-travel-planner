# Reach before monetisation (the funnel order)

**The pattern:** Every monetisation plan we've researched collapses to the same order of operations — and the binding constraint is never the product or the paywall, it's audience. Building checkout before reach is building a shop in a desert.

## The evidence (2026-06-21 spec, grounded in live data)

- Our three pre-revenue shapes are all audience-blocked, not product-blocked: Chanakya (blocker=REACH: 1,360 cold subs, ~24 median views), NakshIQ (blocker=ACTIVATION: ~10-11K visits/mo but ~6 emails), ATLAS (sales execution, out of scope).
- Brutal benchmark: **$5K revenue on a $500 course needs ~666 engaged emails** at 1–2% conversion. A ₹999 Hindi course needs a ~28,000-name list for the same $5K → the leverage is **English + diaspora (Western pricing)** — see [[nri-diaspora-arbitrage]].
- The agreed order (2026-06-21): (1) NakshIQ affiliate flip (built, founder 30-min action) → (2) fix Chanakya reach → (3) lead magnet → ~600–1,000 emails → (4) Anvikshiki Applied course ($99–499, receipts-based positioning). Do NOT build checkout before steps 2–3.
- DOAC/Jay 30-model study (2026-06-19) independently converged: owned audience monetizes its top ~10%; email CPM $50–500 vs YouTube $5–50 → [[owned-audience-funnel]].

## Companion craft lesson (Chanakya reach diagnosis, 2026-06-21)

Shorts reach is gated at the **first second**: the autonomous pipeline regressed Veo motion → Ken-Burns stills and median views fell 1188 → 24 with healthy 68% retention (the retention metric masks the swipe gate). Format, not channel state, was the variable. Fix = restore opening motion; signal threshold = first short to ~200 feed views.
