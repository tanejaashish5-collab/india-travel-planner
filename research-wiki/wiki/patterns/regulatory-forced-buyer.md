# Regulatory forced-buyer (cleanest demand proof)

**The pattern:** a hard dated regulatory deadline + a forced buyer (director liability, license risk, penalty exposure) is the cleanest willingness-to-pay proof that exists — demand needs no persuasion. Both big clean-sheet scouts (2026-06-07/08) independently converged on it.

## Live examples banked (as of June 2026)

- **EU AI Act Annex III** high-risk deadline Aug 2 2026, €35M / 7% penalties → HighRiskWatch (our #1 composite, 3.85)
- **CA AB 2013** AI training-data disclosure, retroactive to 2022, $16–50K penalties → DisclosureForge
- **Australia Payday Super** 1 Jul 2026, 7-day window, director-liable → PaydayReady ("fastest cash 5/5"), SuperGuard
- **India ₹5-crore GST e-invoicing mandate** → BharatBill
- **India-AU organic MRA** (signed Sept 2025, live Jan 2026) → TrueOrganic; **20% MRL export rejections** → PassGuard

## The refinement (2026-06-08 scout)

Highest-scoring STRUCTURE = deadline + forced buyer + **public-but-unreadable dataset**, monetized as **neutral journalism/benchmark, not software** — data-journalism beats SaaS because it's audience-first, AEO-amplified, and needs zero cold sales (which Ashish doesn't do). See [[verification-as-moat]].

## Caveats

- Most of these are 12–24-month first-mover windows, not durable moats — only two-sided networks and compounding proprietary data endure.
- A forced buyer still doesn't override [[passion-fit-gate]] — most regulatory plays scored fit-weak; that tension is why the PURSUE tier sits unactioned.
