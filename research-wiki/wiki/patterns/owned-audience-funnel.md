# Owned-audience funnel

**The pattern:** Free content on platforms you don't own (YT/IG/LinkedIn) exists to move people into a channel you DO own (WhatsApp community, email list). Only the owned layer compounds; algorithmic reach evaporates. Monetization attaches to the owned layer, never the content layer.

## The reference implementation ([[vaibhav-sisinty]], as of 2026-07)

- Every YT video description carries a **sequentially-numbered tracked WhatsApp join link** (`links.stayingahead.com/YT23`, `/YT24`…) — per-video funnel attribution with zero tooling.
- Every IG post — including personal/lifestyle — ends with the identical CTA: "I share updates like these in my free WhatsApp community. Link in bio."
- "Comment 'X' and I'll DM you" posts (7/96 in sample) convert algorithm engagement into DM-list leads.
- The owned layer (1M+ WhatsApp) then feeds B2B training, speaking, courses.

## Status for us (as of 2026-07-01 audit)

- **Chanakya/Anvikshiki: ZERO owned-audience channel** — every view evaporates. Recommended fix: free WhatsApp **Channel** (broadcast — no group moderation overhead), linked from every video/post. Not yet actioned by founder.
- NakshIQ: has email capture (save-trip flow) but no broadcast cadence.
