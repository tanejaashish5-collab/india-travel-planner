# NRI / diaspora arbitrage (verified fact)

**The pattern:** Hindi-language content watched by the 32M-strong diaspora earns **Western CPMs ($10–25) instead of India CPMs ($0.60–2.40)** — and almost nobody executes this faceless. Independently fact-checked TRUE in the 2026-06-11 content radar (source: upGrowth).

## The numbers (as of June 2026)

- AU = $36.21 CPM, world's highest; India direct = $0.60–2.40
- Email CPM $50–500 vs YouTube $5–50 → the arbitrage compounds inside [[owned-audience-funnel]]
- Pricing side (2026-06-21 spec): a $500 course to a Western-priced list needs ~666 emails for $5K; a ₹999 Hindi course needs ~28,000 — **the leverage is English + diaspora pricing**, not Hindi-cheap volume → [[reach-before-monetisation]]
