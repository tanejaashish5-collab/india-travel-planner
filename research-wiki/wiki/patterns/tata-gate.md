# Tata gate (the ethics veto)

**The rule:** every opportunity passes a Ratan-Tata-derived integrity gate as the FINAL word — fail a veto = BLOCKED regardless of conviction or score. Runs automatically in the radar pipeline (`tata-gate-apply.mjs`); a conditional-gate failure makes the fix a mandatory launch precondition.

## The 12 tests

**6 non-negotiable vetoes (weight 3):** no-bribe · don't-exploit-the-weak · survives-public-scrutiny · integrity-is-all-or-nothing · truthful-claims-only · safety-never-compromised.
**6 weighted preferences (1–2★):** patient values-led growth, dignity-of-people, giving-first, etc.
