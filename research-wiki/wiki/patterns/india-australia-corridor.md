# India↔Australia corridor (the un-clonable moat)

**The pattern:** Ashish's Canberra residency + India roots is the one moat nobody can copy — neither an India-side nor an AU-side competitor can run both ends solo. The 2026-06-07 clean-sheet scout (46 agents, 34 ideas) found **7 of its top 10 ideas ride this corridor**, and all 5 of the ledger's GREEN verdicts are corridor or corridor-adjacent.

## The corridor GREEN list (2026-06-07, competitively validated)

- **ShelfBridge** — India brands → Coles/Woolworths shelf (FSANZ + placement + 90-day velocity data); AUD 500–650K Yr3 revenue est.
- **SettleProof** — caregiver pipeline (AU 35K/yr shortfall verified); AUD 1.1–1.5M Yr2 EBITDA est.
- **Antim** — last-journey concierge AU→India (1M diaspora, ~2,000 repatriations/yr)
- **Virasat** — diaspora search-fund-as-service (succession wave: 45% of Indian founders don't expect heirs)
- **PassGuard** — MRL-rejection warranty for exporters (20% rejections = ₹50–80L uninsured/yr)

## Independent corroboration — Antim (2026-07-26)

A Melbourne **business broker** who has read funeral-home P&Ls, speaking on a channel with no stake in the thesis, independently confirms Antim's core economic assumption ([[src-finsol-levelup-scan]]): funeral-sector margins are "amazing", the buyer is uniquely **price-insensitive** ("people would go and bargain on other things, but not on this one"), and **funeral insurance at $25-30K cover already exists** as an attached product in AU. Operator-sourced 2nd witness; Antim's unit economics strengthen, its ranking is unchanged — still outside the four [[passion-fit-gate]] lanes.

The same source also re-measures the corridor's *audience*: the demand spike that drove 71% of that channel's lifetime views came from **first-generation Indian migrants in AU IT jobs, AI/offshoring-threatened, holding $150-200K in offset accounts, asking "what else?"** — the corridor demographic arriving with a measured signal rather than a hypothesis.

## The honest tension

Corridor ideas keep winning on *evidence* and losing on *passion* — most are logistics/compliance shapes outside the four [[passion-fit-gate]] lanes. Unresolved as of 2026-07-07: either a corridor idea inside a passion lane shows up, or one of these gets a partner-operator structure (founder-fit says "India on-ground OK with partner").
