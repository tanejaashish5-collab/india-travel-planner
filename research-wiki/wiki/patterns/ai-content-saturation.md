# AI-content saturation (measured, not vibes)

**The pattern:** "AI tools/news" content is a crowded, visibly fatiguing category as of mid-2026. Even category winners are losing engagement month-over-month.

## Evidence

- [[vaibhav-sisinty]] (2M IG, full-time, category leader): median IG likes fell **~38% May→June 2026** (10.9K → 6.7K, n=49/41); mean fell ~62% (May outlier spikes not reproduced). Measured from Instagram's own GraphQL data, 96-post sample.
- @theromanknox (303K IG, 2026-07-08 check): "70 business ideas" carousel = **68 likes (~0.02% engagement)**; several posts show comments > likes (DM-bait automation). Corollary rule: **audit engagement rate, never follower count** — follower numbers are not evidence ([[src-roman-knox-audit]]).
- Vyan Gandhi finding (2026-06-29): viral AI channels are lead magnets because ad revenue alone doesn't sustain them → [[money-is-services-not-adsense]].
- Consistent with the 2026-06-26 Chanakya pivot research: generic AI/self-help content lanes were crowded; "Anvikshiki Applied" was chosen as the one un-crowded wedge.

- **[[rowan-cheung]] / The Rundown AI (2026-07-27 triage)** — the category's biggest newsletter (claims 2M+ subs, $3M→$7M revenue self-reported to Forbes). Two findings that matter here: (a) **the audience is substantially PAID-acquired** — his own words on beehiiv's blog (direct-fetch verified): *"we plan to move around 50% of our marketing budget from Meta to beehiiv ads"* — the organic core was ~55K from viral X threads in Jan-2023, a **timing arbitrage on the ChatGPT wave that no longer exists**; scale after that was bought. (b) **The category is now recycling its own copy** — his only jobs/AI line is *"AI won't replace you, but people who use AI will,"* the **identical unsourced slogan** [[nate-herk]] opened with on 2026-07-26. Two consecutive triages, same borrowed sentence.
- **Corollary added 2026-07-27:** a huge subscriber/follower number in this category should be assumed **partly purchased** until the acquisition mechanism is shown. "Grew to 2M" and "grew to 2M organically" are entirely different claims, and only the first is ever stated.

- **Third sighting of the same sentence (2026-07-29):** a New York Life executive uses *"AI is not going to take your job, but someone using AI will"* in Anthropic's Financial Services keynote — identical unsourced line to [[nate-herk]] (07-26) and [[rowan-cheung]] (07-27). It has now travelled from creator content into Fortune-100 executive keynotes. The category shares one sentence.

## Implication for us

Gut-check against any future "let's also do an AI-tools channel" idea: the ceiling on that format is compressing for the players who already won it. Differentiated wedges ([[passion-fit-gate]]-compliant, verifiable, un-crowded) only.
