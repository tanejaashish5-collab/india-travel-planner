# src — Medical equipment India, Round 3 (2026-08-02)

**Supersedes the 2026-07-02 kill**, which was Canberra-conditional (*"Your capital fits; your
operating position doesn't"*). Founder lifted the on-ground-India gate 2026-08-02 →
[[rejected-ideas]]. Re-scored from an India position with founder parameters: **capital
₹50L–1cr**, **ramp = "India-based but keep AU income first."** 3 agents. Manufacturing not
re-researched (already killed separately).

## VERDICT: NOT A GO as scoped. One conditional PROBE.

### The genuine unlock — licensing was never the barrier

MD-41 → **MD-42** wholesale licence, State Licensing Authority via CDSCO SUGAM. **₹3,000 per
premise**, 5-year validity, 45–90 days. **"Competent technical staff" is a HIRE, not a founder
qualification** — MDR 2017 accepts a science/engineering diploma/degree or 1–2 years' device
experience (unlike drug licences, which are pharmacist-gated). **A returnee with zero pharma
background can hold it.** OCI can own 100% of an Indian Pvt Ltd; one resident director required
(moot once resident); FEMA Form FC-GPR within 30 days.
⚠️ NCR is **three regulators** (Delhi GNCTD · UP FSDA for Noida/Ghaziabad · Haryana for
Gurugram/Faridabad) — decide before renting. ⚠️ Confirm target reagent SKUs are on the notified
list; some legacy diagnostics still route through the pharmacist-gated drug path.

### Enterability ranking (cold, no network) — inverts the intuition

1. **Home equipment RENTAL** — most enterable (B2C, prepaid working capital, no gate). Unfunded
   proof: Healthy Jeena Sikho, bootstrapped 2015 → 591 employees. **But NCR is the most
   contested city for exactly this model.**
2. **IVD/consumables DEALERSHIP** — enterable, slower. Buyer is a small pathology lab, not a
   hospital committee.
3. **Regional DISTRIBUTORSHIP** — chicken-and-egg: principals appoint on the network you'd need
   the appointment to build; tenders demand 3-yr audited turnover + past-supply certs. Sub-dealer
   path = **3–5+ years**.
4. **ISO/AMC** — least enterable cold; the product *is* trust in your engineers on *this*
   machine. The one scaled public wedge (**BMMP**) has run in 11 states — **not Delhi, UP or
   Haryana**. *(Data may be dated; verify.)*

### The market is STRUCTURALLY COMPRESSING

- **NPPA** already deleted the margin on what a generalist wants: stents (pre-cap **270–1000%**)
  at fixed ceiling; knee implants (distributor+hospital captured **335%**) capped and **renewed
  to Nov 2026**; 6 COVID devices at a **70% trade-margin cap**. A broad all-device Trade Margin
  Rationalisation has "considerable consensus" but is **not yet notified** — every uncapped
  portfolio is one notification away.
- **CDSCO proposal dated 23 June 2026** — hospitals to import **80 Class C/D categories** (MRI,
  CT, PET-CT, mammography) **direct, bypassing licensed importers.** Live, unresolved.
- **PLI is localising** the exact high-value imaging lines (55 products; Siemens MRI in
  Bengaluru) despite 70–80% import dependence.
- **Ceiling is low even at the top:** Trivitron, the largest national distributor (FY24
  ₹525–550cr), runs **mid-single-digit operating margins**. 635 distributors tracked, only 19
  funded. PE rolling up survivors (Everstone's Everlife+Translumina ₹6,000cr; Entero bought 10
  companies in FY25).
- **Distress:** BPL **₹855cr FY26 loss, net worth −85.64%**. Medikabazaar (**$185M raised**) flat
  at $700M since May 2022, CEO ousted twice, auditor resigned, **₹279cr** investor suit.

### 🔴 The finding that matters most — receivables, and you can't escape to private

- **AP Medical Services Corp: payments on hold 4–5 YEARS** → **AiMeD issued a "Caution Red
  Notice"** telling members not to supply without **100% advance**.
- Kerala **₹158.7cr** (supply halted, angioplasty blocked) · Telangana **₹245cr**, some since
  **2022** · Kozhikode **₹75cr**/8 months · GeM system-wide **₹5,488cr**.
- **Haryana: 650 PRIVATE hospitals suspended Ayushman Bharat over ₹490cr**, 4–5 month delays;
  Bihar the same. **Private hospitals awaiting government insurance pass the delay down to
  suppliers — "sell private instead" does not work.**
- You pay principals on 30–60 days against 60–90+ day institutional terms. **You finance both
  legs, and the gap widens as you scale.**
- Outer tail: Gorakhpur 2017 — oxygen supplier cut supply over dues; ≥33 children died in 48h.

### The ethics cost is an exclusion, not a premium

Prosecuted: CBI/RML Delhi 2024 — supplier paid **₹2.48 lakh to a cardiologist "for promoting the
sale of medical equipment"**; Delhi ACB ₹650cr CPA case (X-ray worth ~₹10 lakh bought at ~₹25
lakh); Chhattisgarh ₹660cr. **Under a no-bribe rule, doctor-influenced categories and captured
tender channels are effectively closed** — you lose regardless of price.
*(Evidence hygiene: the agent refused to repeat the "75% of world counterfeits from India"
figure — doesn't trace to WHO, disputed. CDSCO's own figure is ~0.3%.)*

### The one conditional PROBE — IVD reagent-rental to independent NCR labs

Reagents are **~70% of IVD market value**; published case data shows reagent-rental cut
**cost-per-test ~47%** while running **~23% more profitably for the lab** — so you sell better
economics, not a relationship. Not NPPA-capped, not on the CDSCO-80 list, financeable via NBFC
at **10–14% p.a., 3–5 yrs, 70–90% of project cost** collateralised by the equipment.
**$0 test:** one NCR jurisdiction, **25 lab conversations** (past behaviour + concrete
commitments, per [[test-cheap-before-build]]) + written terms from 3 principals.
**Kill gate: <8 of 25 with concrete switching interest AND no principal offering workable terms
→ close the lane permanently.**

### 🔴 The actual blocker is a founder decision, not more research

**"Keep AU income first" is incompatible with every enterable layer.** Rental and IVD both need
on-the-ground operations and field selling in year one; distribution and ISO need more. **The
question is not which layer — it is whether he will work this full-time.** If not, the answer
is no regardless.

### Gaps flagged

No India-specific principal security-deposit figure · no named dealer insolvency attributable to
receivables (mechanism strong, case unproven) · no industry DSO statistic · **the "95% of
hospital purchases via GeM" claim traces to a marketing blog — do not cite** · OEM service
lock-out confirmed for the US (~80%) but **inferred for India** · BMMP state list may be dated ·
no survival base rate exists for Indian device dealerships (MSME/startup figures describe
different populations — do not substitute).
