## Keepers

- **"Trouble selling = trouble storytelling. Don't sell AI — sell the outcome"** (HVAC: you're selling "I pick up the phone"). **Server-on-the-table story**: legal-firm deal won by the vendor who physically plunked a server down — "your data lives here and it won't leave" — while everyone else recited certifications. Demand is never the bottleneck ("not one company I've met is uninterested in AI"); a story the accountable buyer can repeat is.
- **480V line analogy:** dumping Claude credits on employees = handing a factory worker a sparking live line; the value is designing the factory around the new power — *"the hyperscalers can't answer this; if they could they wouldn't have hired thousands of forward-deployed engineers"* → third independent source for the deployment-gap thesis (after [[jason-lemkin]] + the FDE market in [[src-aishwarya-a16z-fde]]).
- **Token-distribution diagnostic:** real teams split ~2B tokens/day passionate adopters vs 2–5M/day proud-account-holders → "what's your token distribution?" = assessment discovery question.
- **Adoption unlock:** ONE challenge that's big + close-in-time + slightly scary + believed → AI becomes the only path; then peer learning spreads it. Team-by-team via handpicked champions; SDM-level team leads are make-or-break; **C-suite must personally use the tools** (IBM 2,000-CEO survey: 76% say leaders need tech fluency — his citation).
- **Cost objection dead:** auto-router, 95% open models (GLM 5.2 class), frontier on demand — rationing tokens = you didn't build a router. (Our Haiku/Sonnet/Opus routing, now a nameable service offering.)
- Context: layoffs = ZIRP overhang + "uncreative boards out of ideas" (w/ Jensen Huang); AI's brand problem → don't lead with "AI" to normie buyers; verification/intent-loop = 2027 investment theme (corroborates the evals positioning); open-source models + leaned-in countries (UAE) decide the future, not 8 lab leaders; households/personal life ~untouched; taste = deliberate zag (Linear → commodified).
