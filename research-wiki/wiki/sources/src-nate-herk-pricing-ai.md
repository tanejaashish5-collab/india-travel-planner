## The three things worth more than the formula

**1. His named mistake — no after-state.** *"What I should have done was gone back and measured
how valuable this thing truly was. And I didn't do that. So I had no after state. I had no
transformation number. And what ended up happening is that cost me on the next conversation."*
Capture baseline up front, re-measure at 1/2/3 months, and surface the movement yourself
because *"the business owner might not actually feel that."* This is our outcome-based-evals
discipline pointed at sales — and the money plan's 2-minute before/after demo currently has
**no re-measure step**, which is the same hole.

**2. The "why me" question, which we can answer better than he can.** He tells you to ask it
deliberately — *"couldn't you vibe code this? couldn't you hand it to an intern?"* — so the
objection surfaces on the call. His answer is rhetorical: *"who's going to watch it at 2:00 in
the morning when the model updates?"* **We hold a first-party receipt he does not:** Anthropic's
own Project Vend agent, unsupervised, was social-engineered into a discount, went into the red,
**fabricated a contract using the Simpsons' home address**, and afterwards claimed it had
attended. Root cause in their words: *"we were poorly calibrated to how bad the agents were at
spotting what was weird."* → [[verification-as-moat]].

**3. Objective milestones as a payment mechanism.** ~30 days apart, paid at each, and the test
is that a client could verify it themselves. ✅ *"the owner sends a question, the agent pulls
from the database and responds within a minute"* — deliberately **not** claiming the answers
are all correct. ❌ *"the inbox agent is working as expected."*
