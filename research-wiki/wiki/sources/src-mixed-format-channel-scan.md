## Format-mixing meta-finding (the actual question asked)

Mixing formats on one handle is fine — Instagram's 2026 guidance recommends a reel+carousel+static weekly mix; sourced consensus says **topic consistency matters more than format consistency** (topic drift, not format drift, is the follow-killer). No causal evidence format-mixing itself grows a channel. India in Pixels proves one-topic-many-formats at 700K+ scale. So "mixed content" is a surface strategy, not a differentiator — the niche does the work.
