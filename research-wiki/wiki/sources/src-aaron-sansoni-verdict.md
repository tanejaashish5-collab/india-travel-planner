# src — Aaron Sansoni / Empire Mastery verdict (2026-08-06)

Founder-supplied Facebook ad ("7 Scaling Secrets", free ebook). **Verdict: DO NOT ENTER THE
FUNNEL** — not because he's a fraud, but because the contract is engineered so that changing your
mind costs you.

## The correction worth keeping

**The "scam report" sites were wrong and I discarded them.** bigscam.org and intelligenceline.com
("1.6/5") made one testable claim — that ProductReview shows "lots of negative reviews" — and
measurement disproved it. **Trustpilot 4.8 (569 reviews, 95% 5★); ProductReview 4.8 (523, 93%
positive).** ~1,090 reviews, two independent platforms, both 4.8. His AFR Young Rich List 2024
placing (**#45, $124m**) also checks out. He is a real, wealthy, listed Australian businessman.

**Method note worth reusing: low-quality scam-aggregator sites are not evidence.** Test one of
their falsifiable claims before citing any of them; here the first test failed.

## Where the risk actually lives — the promoter's own T&Cs

Contracting party is **not Sansoni** — it is **Success Resources Australia Pty Ltd (ABN 12 147 577
866)**, a seminar promoter sitting at **1.4/5, 94% unfavourable**. The 4.8 brand and the 1.4
machine are the same transaction.

- **The venue is declared "their normal place of business"** — which defeats the ACL
  *unsolicited consumer agreement* mandatory cooling-off. A documented structure to remove a
  statutory right, two clauses above *"there is no cooling-off period."*
- **5(b) grants 10 days; 5(d) removes it** — refunds die once the service "is available for you
  to use", and the digital inclusions are available on payment.
- **12 — "Value Return Guarantee" expires at the end of Day 1** of a 3–4 day event, and requires
  asserting the content won't be worth **$500,000 over your business's lifetime**.
- **11(b) entire-agreement** voids whatever the salesperson promised. 5(e) allows 50% retention;
  5(f) gives the seller "final decision".

**Unverified but coherent:** one Verified reviewer states refunds are conditioned on signing a
**confidentiality + non-disparagement deed** — *"which is why their rating is so high."* Could not
corroborate (ProductReview's "Incentivised Review" filter isn't URL-addressable). **Not proven** —
but it would mechanically produce the observed shape: high aggregate, thin tail of very specific
recent complaints.

**ACL counterweight:** "no refunds" terms are not enforceable against consumer guarantees (s64);
unfair-contract-terms carry civil penalties since Nov 2023; s18 misleading conduct survives any
entire-agreement clause. The contract is **more aggressive than it is enforceable**.

## Why it fails for us specifically

Applies [[receipts-over-content]] exactly — *"50–150% top-line growth" and "700,000+ business
owners" are marketing claims attached to a purchase, not receipts.* And it **inverts**
[[operating-position-not-capital]]: the whole promise is buying your way *out* of the operating
position, which the unorganised-sector read found is the one thing that cannot be bought. Product
is knowledge; our constraint is execution and distribution (101 ideas, 0 GREEN).

Ladder: free ebook → call → ~$97 ticket → multi-day event → ~$5,000 programs → reported **$5k/mo
"Inner Circle" on 2-year terms** (~$120k). **Never buy in the room** — phone purchase carries 10
days (5(b)), in-room carries none (5(a)).
