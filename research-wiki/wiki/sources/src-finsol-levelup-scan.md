# src-finsol-levelup-scan

Founder-pasted channel (`@finsol.insurance`, 2026-07-26): "any interesting ideas here to build business etc." Priyanka Shah, Melbourne insurance broker; `LevelUp` guest podcast as lead-gen for her own brokerage. **13th lead-magnet-machine data point**, smallest scale seen, and the most honest — the podcast IS the funnel, no course/community/template.

## Base rate (33/33 videos, yt-dlp)

710 subs · 33 videos over 22 months · **median 127 views** · 24 of 33 under 500. Two years of consistent fortnightly output with no distribution engine = ~127 views. Useful calibration number.

**One outlier = 71% of all channel views ever:** "7 Businesses That Actually Make Money in Australia" (`Rd2_mJc0gUQ`, 2026-07-08, 63 min) — **29,005 views**, 273 likes, 44 comments, **228× median**. Guests: Vaibhav Grover (business broker, Next Goal Advisory) + Hardik Shah (mortgage broker, Loan Gallery), contacts published by the channel. Like rate 0.94% reads organic, but a lone 228× spike on 710 subs is also the shape of paid promotion — **UNCONFIRMED**, treat as strong signal not proof.

## Verified facts worth keeping (brokers' own on-record numbers)

- **AU small-business multiples by effort, not by profit**: Tatts Lotto / post office ≈4× EBITDA · play centre 2-2.5× · cafe 1-1.5×. Same $200K profit sells for $600-800K as a lotto kiosk vs ~$300K as a cafe. Effort is the whole spread.
- **Business acquisition finance**: unsecured, banks want **50% deposit** (65% LVR for Australia Post/IGA/McDonald's/Subway), **10-12%** rates, deposit can be property equity, and **buyer operating experience is a hard filter** ("truck driver opening a cafe — bank may not support").
- **Traps named**: laundromat ($400K buys $50K/yr, 8-yr payback, 10-yr machine life) · automated car wash (modelled case needed 60 cars/day to cover CBD rent) · hand car wash ("not a car wash, it's an HR business") · petrol stations (EV + automated company sites) · NDIS licences sold at $50K are worthless without providers.
- **Seller fraud signatures**: petrol station scanning one cigarette box/day to inflate takings; veggie shops claiming "50% cash sales". Brokers run 2-week in-business trials; accountants "go by the P&L, don't understand how a business is running".
- **Silent-investor syndicate structure**: 4-5 investors × $50-100K into play centres/child care/laser clinics, monthly P&L reports, optional weekend shifts, investors often buy outright at 12-18 months. Quoted 12-15% (to "20-25%") returns, unaudited. Apprenticeship-with-yield.

## The actual signal (not the video's title)

The 29K came from **first-generation Indian migrants in AU IT jobs watching AI/offshoring eat their roles, sitting on $150-200K in an offset account, asking "what else?"** — the host and brokers return to this framing repeatedly. Same demographic as [[india-australia-corridor]], same situation as the founder's own (contract ended 2026-03-30). Demand signal arrives measured, not hypothesised.

## Verdicts

- ✅ **GO, no build — guest on the podcast as the AI operator.** The channel already ran its AI episode (`owZ9TAIY08M`, Mia Bauer / Automate Social, 2026-02-12) and it did **103 views** — generic custom-GPT/5-tools content, no receipts. The 2026-07-26 demo pack is exactly what it lacked. Cost = one email (`priyanka@finsolinsurance.com.au`) — **full draft ready to send** in the source doc §6, leads with her own numbers, names the failed AI episode without insulting it, offers the demo before the ask, bridges AI-fear to income-protection (her own funnel); do NOT attach the demo pack on first contact. Guest roster = warm network of the AU professional-services SMBs in [[src-money-plan-2026-07-22]]. Unparks [[distribution-arbitrage-via-guesting]]. Kill gate: no reply in 14 days → same pitch to 3 other Indian-Australian finance channels, then stop.
- ❌ **NO-GO — AI due-diligence tool for business buyers.** Vivid pain, but (a) crowded at the tooling layer (10+ AI-M&A-diligence vendor guides in 2026) and (b) **the broker owns the client** — Grover's literal closing CTA was "book a consulting with a business advisor". Selling software to people who meet a gatekeeper first. A public premise broadcast to 29K people → [[public-premises-are-pre-arbitraged]].
- ❌ **NO-GO — buying one of these businesses.** Best-evidenced item in the episode, fails [[passion-fit-gate]] (none of the 4 lanes), fails the banks' own experience filter, and 50% of $600-800K isn't the current position.
- 📌 **Corroboration, no action — [[india-australia-corridor]] / Antim.** An AU broker who has read funeral-home P&Ls calls the margins "amazing" and the buyer uniquely price-insensitive ("people bargain on other things, but not on this one"); funeral insurance at $25-30K cover already exists as an attached product. Independent operator-sourced 2nd witness for Antim's unit economics. Ranking unchanged (still fails passion-fit).

## Meta
