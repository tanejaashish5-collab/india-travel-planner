# src — Delhi premium childcare / preschool gate (2026-08-02)

Founder-originated idea: a specialised premium childcare in Delhi modelled on **FAPS**
(French-Australian Preschool, Canberra), plus a direct question about **subsidised government
land**. 3 agents; primary land law pulled directly (dda.gov.in, edudel.nic.in, indiacode).
**Zero prior art** — the 101-idea ledger and graveyard had 0 matches for
preschool/childcare/creche/daycare in India. Genuinely unscouted, now scouted.

## VERDICT: NO GO on the FAPS-equivalent thesis

### 1. The premise is false — but the instinct was location-specific and correct

Every pedagogy assumed missing already operates in Delhi NCR: **Waldorf** (UKTI, est. 2013;
Aarambh; Banyan Steiner) · **Reggio Emilia** (Serra, Amiown/Amity, Little Millennium) ·
**forest school** (Forest Spirit, Gurugram) · **IB PYP** (Pathways, GD Goenka Global).

**Footprints is not small:** 232–240+ centres, 26–30 cities, **$7.5M Series A** (Tanglin),
IIT-IIM founders, HighScope curriculum, live CCTV + parent app. **But Sector 51 Gurgaon sits at
1★ on MouthShut while other locations run 4.7–4.9★.** Same brand, opposite execution. **The
evidenced weakness is franchise quality control, not the category** — and Kido's India CEO names
it: *"Growing too fast has been the nemesis for many a childcare brand… not able to control
quality with their steep growth."* **KLAY ($32.6M) stays company-owned specifically to avoid it;
Kido acquired Amelio rather than franchise.** The obvious fix is already funded and executing.

Capital is arriving: **KKR ~₹1,760cr into EuroKids/Lighthouse (Nov 2025)** with Canada's PSP ·
**Dibber (Norway)**, 600+ schools in 9 countries, publicly planning **100 more India centres in
3 years** · Kido (UK, $7.5M) · Maple Bear (Canada).

### 2. The FAPS model cannot be reproduced — the subsidy is the whole difference

FAPS: not-for-profit, **132 places**, owns its Red Hill site, teachers hold the French
*Professeur des Écoles* (**Master's-equivalent**; Australia's statutory minimum is Cert III),
rated **Exceeding** on Physical Environment. Australia mandates **and audits** 1:4 / 1:5 / 1:11
ratios and **3.25 m² indoor + 7 m² outdoor** unencumbered per child.

ACT average **$150.36/day** × FAPS's own 40-week calendar ≈ **₹20.1 lakh/yr sticker**; a family
actually pays ~**₹4–7 lakh** after Child Care Subsidy. **The ₹13–16 lakh per child per year in
between is the Australian government** (CCS/ECEC spend ≈ 70–80% of sector revenue). No Indian
equivalent exists.
*(Precision: FAPS's own Productivity Commission submission says it receives no direct grant —
but it is **CCS-approved**, so its families claim the demand-side subsidy. Institution
unsubsidised, demand heavily subsidised. FAPS does not publish fees; ₹20.1L is an ACT proxy.)*

**Two consequences worth carrying forward:**
- **Rent is NOT the constraint** — at premium Gurugram rates against 35 sq ft/child, rent is
  **₹5,250–6,300/child/month** against fees of ₹12,500–25,000. **Ratios and the missing subsidy
  are what make it unaffordable, not property.**
- **No regulatory moat.** India's 1:20 / 1:10 is a **draft 2013 policy**, unenforced, with **no
  outdoor requirement at all**. In Australia the NQF forces rivals to match you; in India nothing
  does. Premium must be defended by brand alone — permanently.

**Red flags at scale:** Lighthouse Learning FY25 net profit **collapsed ₹186cr → ₹8cr** on 34%
revenue growth; Zee Learn **EBITDA CAGR −55%**.

### 3. Subsidised land — asked directly, answered directly: NO, and it's a trap

DDA's Nazul Rules 1981 Category (e) is literally **"Nursery, Private, Middle and Senior
Secondary Schools"** — 176 nursery societies hold such land, almost all allotted **2001–2004**.
**But DDA's own site states today: "Allotment / Disposal of land with regard to schools are under
review"** (verified live 2026-08-02); schools were excluded from the 21st Phase mega e-auction
that ran for other categories.
⚠️ **Trap:** DDA archive entries "Nursery at Andheria More" / "Dwarka" (Dec 2025) are
**Horticulture plant nurseries in parks** (listed beside "Flower shop at Millennium Park") — not
school land. Do not misread as the channel reopening.

**The price is the business model, not money:** non-profit Society/Trust/Section 8 only (a
for-profit company **cannot** hold it; DSER **Rule 50** conditions recognition on *"not run for
profit to any individual"*, with DoE book inspection) · **25% EWS free seats as a lease
condition**, Delhi HC-enforced, breach → cancellation · non-transferable 10 years, mortgage needs
the **Lieutenant Governor's** consent · **growth locked out** (*"classes running beyond
pre-primary are not permissible"*) · fee regulation under the no-commercialisation doctrine
(*Unnikrishnan*, *T.M.A. Pai*, *Modern School*) · **breach = resumption, zero compensation** ·
ground rent 2.5% of premium/yr after 5 years. And it's an **e-auction** anyway (comparable
reserves ₹52,000–79,000/sqm; 2026 bids averaged **2.75× reserve**).

**The asymmetry that decides it:** Delhi pre-schools are **practically unregulated** — RTE covers
6–14 only, NEP 2020's ECCE push isn't binding law, and only AP/Jharkhand/TN/Maharashtra have
operative regimes (**Delhi doesn't**). *"There is no strict implementation of registration rules"*
(Bar & Bench). **That is precisely why the entire organised industry rents at market rate as
ordinary for-profit companies. Taking government land volunteers you into the whole non-profit
regime.**

**Foreign ownership:** 100% FDI in education is automatic-route, **but FDI is prohibited into
trusts and societies**; Section 8 triggers FCRA ("quite unattractive"). Industry-standard
**three-entity structure**: non-profit holds recognition/land; a for-profit owns the real estate
and leases to it; a for-profit services co takes management fees. **FCRA does not touch fee
income** (Explanation 3 to s.2(1)(h)). ⚠️ **OCI trap: a donation *from* an OCI is foreign
contribution (an NRI's is not)** — fund via fees/equity, never a personal donation.
⚠️ **Tata-gate note:** that structure exists to extract profit from a legally non-profit system.
Combined with *subsidised public land plus a 25% free-seat obligation*, ask whether it survives
the front-page test. Renting commercially raises no such question.

### What survives

**(a) Franchise-quality wedge** — real, but KLAY and Kido are already executing it with capital
he doesn't have. **(b) Commercial third-language immersion** — no open-enrolment, Indian-priced
French/German/Mandarin preschool appears to exist (Lycée Français charges **€3,960–5,280 in
registration alone**; Alliance Française doesn't teach under age 9). **This is
absence-of-supply, NOT proof-of-demand** — zero evidence of Indian families asking. $0 test:
catchment ads → waitlist; **kill gate <25 genuine enquiries in 14 days.**
