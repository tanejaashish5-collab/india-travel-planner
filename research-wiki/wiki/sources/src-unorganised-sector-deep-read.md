## ScrapUncle (Mukul Chhabra) — e-commerce in reverse

₹22cr at ₹80cr; 70 e-pickups in NCR; 1-acre Brijwasan facility. **Contribution nets to ~zero:**
sale AOV ₹1,500 − purchase ₹900 − collection ₹300 − warehousing/last-mile ₹250 − CAC ₹50.
Cause is **~50% vehicle utilisation** (15 orders/day capacity, running 6–7); at full density
₹300/pickup halves to ₹150 — **and ₹150 is the whole margin**. Burn ₹90L→₹50L monthly on ₹2.5cr
revenue. Warehousing is *not* the cost centre (₹15–16L/mo); logistics is (**₹70K/vehicle/mo**).
Retention >50%, 2–3 sales/year, **LTV:CAC ≈ 9:1**.

**The real asset:** there is no Walmart layer to inherit — no barcoding, warehousing or 3PL for
scrap exists anywhere. Can't outsource collection (30–40 min in-home transaction, closer to
Urban Company than delivery) or warehousing (no one wants scrap in their facility). Infinite SKU
forced into a taxonomy. He is building six companies at once — the moat and the burn, same fact.
**Consumer wedge is trust:** the local kabaadi cheats on the scale, so they offer a **₹1 lakh
guarantee** on scale accuracy. Gig partners own nothing (company supplies truck, scale,
charging, maintenance); picker ₹30–35K/mo, helper ₹18K.

## Horizon Reclaim (Mohit Bajaj) — the qualification moat

Family rubber footwear since 1975 (Bata OEM). Plant 2006 at Bhagwanpur, Roorkee — a deliberate
**state-formation subsidy arbitrage** (Uttarakhand formed 2003): cheap industrial power + ~30%
capital subsidy on machinery + excise exemption. Capex ₹5–6cr at 75% bank / 25% own;
**debt-free by 2011**, still no working-capital or vehicle loan. Revenue ~₹50cr, **IPO planned
at ₹200–250cr ≈ 5× revenue**.

Outputs: crumb (→ rubberised roads), reclaim sheet (→ substitutes virgin rubber), pyrolysis oil
(by-products: carbon → cement kilns, the only boilers licensed for hazardous waste; high-grade
radial steel at a premium).

**Moat 1 — qualification, not price.** 20-year customer bank paying on his terms: *"You could
give them cheaper, better material and they won't take it… I will not risk my grip."* Derived
from **YKK zips at 20–25% margins** — a brand cannot take asymmetric downside. Switching cost is
**re-qualification risk**. [[verification-as-moat]] in industrial form.
**Moat 2 — self-built machinery** (only 20–25% bought outside). Product is identical across
players; the business is *conversion charges*, so machine R&D **is** the margin. Proof: doubled
Unit 1 capacity in one month, sold it the same month at a **higher** margin with the same
headcount.

**Regulatory tailwind:** 2022 gazette recognised recycling; under **EPR** ~10 credit-buyers face
~5 recyclers. Horizon was **among the first two licensed to import tyres** into India. Australia
banned whole-tyre export (2021) so material arrives pre-shredded; Horizon scoped an Australian
facility in 2023. Reclaim machinery is **made nowhere but India (Gujarat)**.
