## The 5 that stood out (bearing on the money plan)
1. **Sell the efficiency outcome, not the tool** — his hiring test (60-min hospital gender-ratio question; winners ask the receptionist; "none of the answers required AI") + explicit warning against "which AI tools do you use". Independent validation of the **demo pack** built the same day; plus the MBA/placement line (name the ONE outcome the buyer pays for).
2. **Trust Quadrant** — integrity/consistency/competence/benevolence, a visible gap in ANY kills trust. Explains why the governance one-pager beats a capability pitch.
3. **Government = a protected profit pool** ("a very large chunk of public-market profit pools are protected by regulation/licence… impact not felt as quickly") → **don't sell adopt-or-die urgency to the APS, sell assurance/risk-ownership**; real urgency exists only commercially, via **revenue-per-employee game theory**.
4. **"Audit, don't decide"** — AI to audit judgment, never to originate decisions, else "the brain will atrophy" = his sentence for *agents draft, humans own*. Plus a stealable anti-sycophancy prompt ("five ways I can ask better questions vs the top 1% — identify my gaps").
5. **Resourceful > Insightful** — clients rely on who *fixes*, not who *explains* → pitch-register correction across the pack + STAR stories.

## Other keepers
Profession bifurcation (AI 10x's the top, median wage compresses, tail captures more) · "Indian traffic" analogy for why large orgs show no AI gain + CRED ~90% AI-written code + "10% of employees becoming a different species" · **free-work exception to value-per-hour** (corroborates the free-first sprint in [[src-money-plan-2026-07-22]]) · sales = shamelessness + homework · **decisive kill: "not one company makes >$10M selling software to Indian businesses"** (they hire three more people) → no India-SMB tool lane · psychology (envy is hyper-local "like wifi"; reputation = $1 deposits/total withdrawals; shame-bug; copy end-states-not-journeys; Gangotri health/prosperity/status filter) · CRED's 5 values + ex-founder hiring + exceeds/meets/does-not-meet appraisal.
