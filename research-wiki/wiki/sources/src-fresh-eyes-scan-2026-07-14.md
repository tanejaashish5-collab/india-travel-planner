6. Data licensing pivoting to real-time feeds (Microsoft Publisher Content Marketplace Feb 2026); only ~4/10 licensing deals still include training rights.
7. Agentic travel live and trust-starved: Sabre/PayPal/Mindtrip end-to-end booking launched Q2 2026, but only 8% of travelers trust AI to book (Expedia 2026) — scarce: ground-truth human-verified local data at safety granularity.
