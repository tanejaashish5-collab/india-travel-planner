## The meta-finding

Combined with the [[passion-fit-gate]]'s 2026-07-12 refill (8/8 CROWDED, opposite method — profile gate ON, creator-informed), two consecutive full idea-generation runs using opposite search methods produced identical results: ~106 validated ideas across the ledger, zero open gaps at the idea level. Reasoning correctly from *public* premises (facts every funded founder in 2026 also has) converges on ground funded competitors already reached 12–24 months earlier. Three of the nine were killed specifically by **governments already shipping the product free** — NSW Energy Switch, the Australian Death Notification Service — the strongest possible form of "someone's already doing it." Full reasoning promoted to a new pattern: [[public-premises-are-pre-arbitraged]].

**Practical consequence banked in the source doc:** stop varying the search *method* — the next run, if any, must invert direction and enumerate PRIVATE premises first (NakshIQ's verified corpus, the Chanakya channel, the 15-business automation OS, India-AU dual presence, NV1/senior-BA domain knowledge) and derive only ideas impossible without them.
