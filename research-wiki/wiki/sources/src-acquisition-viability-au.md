# src — Can we buy a business cheap? AU acquisition viability (2026-08-06)

Founder question after [[src-sansoni-funnel-teardown]]: *"how could we buy the business as well for
cheap — isn't that the whole game he teaches."* Full evidence gate run: three research legs against
ATO / ASIC / Fair Work / NSW Small Business Commissioner / AICD / business.gov.au / RBA / APH, plus
IBBA and Stanford base rates, plus **132 real current listings** from three AU marketplaces.

**Answer: yes, that's the game; the technique is real; it does not work here, and least of all for
him.** New pattern: [[no-money-down-is-a-security-swap]].

## Where he's right (state this first — it's the credible part)

Vendor finance, earn-outs, deferred settlement, LBO-against-target-assets are all real, standard
M&A. **His multiples were accurate** — he claimed 2–3× owner-operator / 3–5× under-management;
132 real listings came back **median 2.0×, range 1.1–5.2×**. The retirement wave is real
(magnitude inflated 2–3×, see [[src-sansoni-funnel-teardown]]).

## The five findings that end it

1. **Australia has no SBA.** The whole US playbook rests on the SBA 7(a) guaranteeing **up to 90%**
   of a purchase (canonical 80/10/10, 10% buyer cash). No Australian equivalent exists — accountants
   are *petitioning* for one. **AU lenders want 20–40% down** and lend poorly against goodwill. On a
   $250K business that's **$50K–$100K cash**, not zero.
2. **Vendor finance is rare here.** **0 of 132** live listings advertised it (caveat: it's
   negotiated, not advertised). Measured on *completed* deals, IBBA Market Pulse: seller notes in
   **≤15%**, sellers still take **~84% cash at close**. The ~70% figure that circulates is **US**.
3. **What replaces the cash is your signature** — lease PG (and *"a company or trust structure may
   not protect a guarantor"*), a GSA over your company **and assets** for vendor finance, franchisor
   /supplier guarantees — **plus statutory exposure requiring no signature**: DPNs for PAYG/GST/super
   (**no grace period** on post-appointment debt; 13,648 DPNs / $1.88b in six months of 2023) and
   s588G insolvent trading (unlimited compensation, bankruptcy, disqualification, up to 5 yrs
   imprisonment where dishonesty). **A $0-down deal loads debt service from day one, which raises
   the insolvent-trading risk.**
4. **There is no $0 version.** DD + legal + stamp duty = **5–15% of price**, uncancellable. The
   VSL's *"no lawyers, no accountants"* (26:42) is exactly the saving that creates the exposure —
   forensic DD exists to reconcile stated profit against lodged returns and BAS against bank
   deposits.
5. **Base rates:** **1-in-15** prospective buyers ever close · **19–20 months** search · **~100
   reviewed per acquisition** · **37%** buy rate inside the best paid program (self-reported). And
   the **selection problem**: heavy seller finance correlates with businesses that couldn't clear a
   lender's bar. bsale: *"you can buy a business in this instance with no money down. But the
   business probably isn't successful."*

## Two things that end it for him specifically

- **India relocation** — **119 of 132** listings are location-locked; only **5** location-independent.
  Buying an Australian corner shop is buying a reason to stay in Australia.
- **Needs capital AND ~2 years of income** to search unpaid through ~100 businesses.
- Bonus, from held scrapes: **Codie Sanchez's own step one** (DOAC, `yt-IYu_PDPqKFc`) is *maximise
  your salary first, then buy on the side.* The category's biggest name says get a job first.

## What IS open

**Digital assets** — drops the entire liability stack (no lease, no payroll, no DPN exposure, no
stamp duty), location-independent (works from India), ~2× profit / 2–4× revenue.

**The sharpest version:** the best asset to buy is one with **the defect you have already personally
fixed**. NakshIQ is at **918 sessions/wk → 8 confirmed subscribers → 16 newsletters published to
those 8**. Fix that, and the fix becomes the acquisition edge — converting a bet on a method into
[[receipts-over-content]] applied to yourself. Sequence: fix capture → prove it with a number →
*then* buy that defect cheaply. Which is his own model's order — **list first, sell second** — and
makes [[map-not-territory-lead-magnet]] the prerequisite, not a competing task.
