## Method

This run inverts: enumerate what the founder PRIVATELY owns, derive only ideas that *require* those assets, then refute-mode validate with two extra tests — (a) does a government or platform vendor already do this free? (b) premise test: is the asset a real wedge against found incumbents, or replicable by a funded team in a quarter?
