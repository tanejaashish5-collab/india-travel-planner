## 9 operating principles (compressed)

1. **Optimize for the revenue-generating activity** — recurring question: "is that a revenue-generating activity, or are you optimizing the wrong thing?" Funnel order: sales calls → onboarding → fulfillment; fix the top first.
2. **Qualify at the END of the sales call**, not before — any interested reply gets a calendar link, no pre-judging one-liners.
3. **Don't break the chain** — Seinfeld-method whiteboard X-calendar for a deliberately sub-maximal daily deliverable (3 Upwork apps/day, not 30). Spawned ~100 "Justin lookalikes" inside the community.
4. **You don't know your limit until you hit it** — Justin kept selling until delivery slipped 2 weeks → 2 months, then backed off. Capacity is discovered, not estimated.
5. **The Dip** (Seth Godin) — 3-4 months of zero revenue while doing the right daily actions; nearly quit; revenue then jumped.
6. **Retainer maturation** — hours → hours-plus-deliverables (strategy call, Slack access) → results-based, shifting internal hourly up without changing the sticker.
7. **Simplify → build → scale**, in that order — half the time a process change removes the need for automation entirely.
8. **First 5-10 clients are not for money** — they're for reviews, testimonials, referrals, skills.
9. **Most buyers are "10 years behind"** — pen-and-paper CRMs — so simple Make.com systems still sell fine. Paired with Nick's own caveat, on camera: "our business model is definitely special. In another business model you might achieve a quarter of that."
