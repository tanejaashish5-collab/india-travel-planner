## The offer

$999 "AI Tools Assessment" for small business owners (2–20 staff, $500K–5M revenue). 45-min structured interview → prescribe 3–7 off-the-shelf tools → guarantee: find ≥5 reclaimable hrs/week or 100% refund (avg claimed 7 hrs/wk, ~$60/mo tool cost → 4-figure monthly net ROI pitch). ~2–3 hrs total fulfilment/client. Claimed 50–60% convert to implementation work; claimed LTV $3–10K.

## Four phases

1. **Discovery call** (45 min, recorded, AI note-taker) — probe only, never prescribe on call 1. Core questions: walk me through yesterday · what do you dread · where does work pile up · what did you try to automate and fail · magic wand, delete one process (most common answer: email).
2. **AI analysis** — transcript → a Claude skill mines pain points + researches tools (directories: futurepedia.io, theresanaiforthat.com). Improves via exemplar-feedback loop (first 2–3 reports 60–70% right, by #5 near copy-paste). QA is human — caught Claude prescribing Salesforce to a 4-person landscaper, subbed a small-biz CRM instead.
3. **The report** — 7 slides, iterated 12×, "a confused mind doesn't buy/implement/upsell": ① title ② executive summary (1–2 pains, hours reclaimed, primary lever money/time/quality) ③ effort-vs-impact matrix (top-left quick wins = the report; top-right major projects = the upsell seed) ④ quick-wins summary (pain → tool) ⑤ recommended solutions (tool · pain · $/mo · setup time · hrs saved — 50% of the review call lives here) ⑥ 4-day quick-start plan (10 min/day, kills overwhelm) ⑦ financial impact (hrs × hourly rate − tool cost) + next steps.
4. **Review call** (30 min) — walk the report, then three closing questions: which is most urgent · DIY or want help · what's your timeline → the implementation upsell asks itself.

## Upsell menu (his prices)

Process redesign $3–3.5K (fix the process before automating — map current→future state, a separate engagement from automation) · simple automation builds $1.5K (Zapier/n8n class) · knowledge systems (custom GPT trained on a business broker's 400–500 repeat-buyer emails → response time ~0) · custom workflows (Claude skills + a maintenance retainer, since processes change) · full implementation ~$8K bundles.

## The star upsell — AI Concierge retainer

2× 45-min screen-share/month teaching the client Claude Cowork + building skills WITH them; unlimited Voxer access, 12-business-hour SLA — **near-zero real load, very high perceived value** (3 messages total across 5 clients in 2.5 months: the asymmetry IS the product). Priced $1,200 → $1,500 → $1,800 → $2,000/mo, raising the price on every new close; hard cap at 6 clients = legitimate scarcity, used in every sales call. Effective rate ≈ $1,000/hr. Session method = **AOA: Audit (show me how you do it) → Optimize (13 steps → 7) → Automate (make it a Claude skill)**, rinse-repeat per workflow. First call = onboarding plugin only (connect tools, context files, global instructions, one scheduled task, one skill). Retention devices: a 90-day-win question on the intake form (4 of 5 hit inside 60 days → remind + upsell) + a Notion "everything we accomplished" inventory emailed after every call.

## Seven acquisition methods (all warm/local, zero capital, zero audience)

His framing: "99% won't do these, that's why they work."
1. Local "AI for business" meetup — barter a co-working space's conference room for the foot traffic; 20-min "how to use Claude" talk; collect contacts at the door; follow up in 24h. Compounds like SEO (clients come from event #3–6). Combine with #5 (co-host in a plugged-in realtor's/accountant's office).
2. Door knocking — 30 doors → 5 meetings → 2 clients (a listener's DM'd result).
3. LinkedIn DMs, local-only — "I live down the street" hook; probe pain, never pitch message one; voice notes/Looms.
4. Free 15-min mini-audits for your own network — prescribe ONE tool free; full assessment is the upsell; de-risk framing "worst case you learn two tools."
5. Agency partners — accountants/insurance agents/realtors/coaches whose clients ask AI questions they can't answer; referral fee; follow up every 2–3 weeks; co-branded workshops.
6. AI office hours at a co-working space, weekly, free — plugs directly into ICP (one community member's result: 10 asks → 2 spaces → $175 bartered door prizes → 11 attendees → 2 follow-ups).
7. Post your wins / build in public — a "win" can be one good answer to one ChatGPT question; manufacture cadence early (90 days of one-prompt-a-day carousels).

## Pricing psychology worth stealing

**Credit-the-assessment** — apply the $999 fee as a credit toward the implementation price, after marking that price up by $1K first, so the "discount" nets to only $1 of real cost to him but reads to the client as a full assessment-fee waiver. **Tiered spot scarcity** (Greg's own line: "4 spots at $800/mo, then $1,200, then $1,600"). The money-back guarantee costs nothing when the bar is "find 5 hrs/week" for any 2–20-person business — it is close to unloseable.
