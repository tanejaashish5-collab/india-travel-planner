**India's largest Hindi self-improvement / manifestation YouTube funnel. 2.71M subscribers, 994

## The strategic drift — the transferable read

**Skills built the channel; manifestation monetises it.** His top four videos (~38M views combined)
are **English fluency, communication, sales, spending** — teachable, falsifiable, outcome-bearing.
His 2026 output is **manifestation, affirmations, mantras, 369 codes, numerology**, plus news
commentary that performs worst of all (1.6K–4.7K views).

Skills content is what grew it. Unfalsifiable content is what sells ₹75,000–₹5,90,000 retreats.
**The reach decline tracks the drift.**

## The ladder — ₹2,500 to ₹5,90,000

Free YouTube → free 5-day Law of Attraction program / free "Magical Morning" → free "Mass Healing
Session" (**15 min, 4× daily, 28 days** — a habit installer, not a lead magnet) → 2-day physical
Manifestation Retreat (Pune/Ahmedabad/Raipur) → high ticket.

Top of the ladder: **Train The Trainer Mastermind 2026 — ₹5,00,000** and **100X Growth Mastery —
₹5,90,000**, plus "Coach to Highly Paid Coach". **The most expensive thing he sells is the means to
become him** — the third independent confirmation of the [[receipts-over-content]] inversion
(*a revenue figure from someone selling you the means to copy it is a price tag*), after
[[nitin-joshi]] and [[src-sansoni-funnel-teardown]].

**No price appears anywhere public.** Every event routes to a Google Form; the number is quoted by
a human on a call. The gate is the price, not the content.
