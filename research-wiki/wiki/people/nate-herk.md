# Nate Herk

AI-automation YouTuber (~845K subs as of 2026-07), runs an AI agency + paid Skool community ("AI Automation Society"). Our most-scraped teacher — six separate studies, and the recurring verdict is **"we already run most of what he teaches; take 1–3 pieces per study."**

## The six studies

1. **2026-06-23 "next-step business"** — his advice validated against our constraints: "AI consultant" lane CROWDED + fails [[passion-fit-gate]]; paid Hindi edu DYING (Warikoo shut ₹100cr business, 2026).
2. **2026-06-26 "Claude Code make me money"** (238K views) — 4 upgrades: Council/ROAST anti-sycophancy panel, build-then-verify (NYU: ~40% of Copilot code had security holes), context-rot management, separate-evaluator subagents (parallel agents beat single 90%+, Anthropic internal). We already ran ~80%; the ONE gold piece: **a /council skill** — 6 personas → verdict (green-light/reshape/kill) + cheapest 48h test. Status: `.claude/commands/council.md` exists (untracked) as of 2026-07.
3. **2026-07-07 Karpathy LLM-Wiki** — the pattern this very wiki implements; audit said we already ran ~70% (memory system = wiki). His own advice: **Fable is overkill for ingestion, use Opus.**
4. **2026-07-08 "Opus as Fable"** (published the day the free window closed) — keep Fable's process, not its intelligence. ~75% already ours: his headline test (Fable/Opus orchestrator + Sonnet/Haiku workers ≈ all-expensive, 3× cheaper) was our codified rule since 2026-04-27, pre-Fable. Taken: the 5-gate discipline-skill idea (scope→evidence→attack→verify→report), a consolidated model+effort routing table, and the **xhigh/max-overthinks** effort nuance → [[claude-code-ecosystem]]. *Correction 2026-07-14: checked against Anthropic's official docs — the overthink failure mode is real, but the fix is an explicit act-when-ready prompting line, not a blanket drop to high; xhigh remains Anthropic's own recommended default for coding/agentic work. Also fixed: his "Fable-low ≈ Opus-high" was ≈ prior-gen Opus 4.7-xhigh, not our current 4.8. Detail: [[src-fable-parity-stack-2026-07-14]].*
5. **2026-07-09 "One prompt, a whole company"** (video published 2026-07-08) — a single goal-file prompt let Fable orchestrate a complete company (Counterbrief, a chargeback-evidence SaaS) in 3–4 hours, every subagent Opus or Sonnet, zero Fable workers. Taken: the launch-package-factory goal file ([[src-nate-one-prompt-company]]) — converts one PURSUE-tier idea into a presell smoke-test asset kit; his confessed gap (stress-test the idea upfront) is exactly what our radar/strategist/tata-gate pipeline already does.
6. **2026-07-15 "The $200K AI Job That Didn't Exist Last Year"** (published 2026-07-14, 21.3K views day one) — thesis: value shifts from the external AI-agency seat to the in-house AI consultant seat; a 4-step career roadmap (audit own job → automate + prove with numbers → make proof visible → attack constraints, formalize the role). **8th lead-magnet-machine data point** — free-community funnel, idea-value zero, same as every prior study. What's actually new: the video independently corroborates our banked agency-squeeze evidence (his own words: "the exact same thing that made AI agencies a ton of money is about to replace them") from a totally different angle than our own audits — a competing creator's career thesis, not a self-audit. The genuinely useful artifact came from the research session's own follow-on work, not the video: a 3-layer/4-delivery-model framework for how our OS would actually get implemented for a client. Detail: [[src-nate-herk-200k-inhouse-ai-job]].
7. **2026-07-23 "5 Hacks to Level Up Your AI OS"** (25 min, 929 views, TRIAGE ~85–90% banked) — his 5 hacks are all things we already run at a more mature level: (1) CLAUDE.md-as-router = our exact 3-layer boot/dashboard-index structure; (2) *have AI audit its own OS* = our dreaming job + capability-watcher + daily-QA; (3) crons-to-update-data = our whole launchd/CronCreate fleet; (4) segment knowledge = research-wiki folders + per-project memory; (5) *backtrack* (make the agent diagnose WHY it failed to find data, then fix routing — not "don't make mistakes") = the one small prompting micro-tactic worth adopting. His context vocabulary (4 failure modes poisoning/bloat/confusion/clash · expertise-vs-situational context) is clean articulation of things our rules already enforce (no-fabrication=anti-poison, MEMORY 17KB cap=anti-bloat, verify-before-verdict=anti-confusion, freshness-drift cron=anti-clash). **The take wasn't from the video — it was applying his hack #2 to Ashish's real OS**: a routing-integrity audit found research-wiki CLEAN (0 real broken links/94 pages) but the memory index drifted (48 orphan files; the live one = an unactioned 2026-07-19 dream proposal). Detail: [[src-nate-herk-ai-os-5-hacks]].

8. **2026-07-26 "This AI Technology Will Replace Millions (Here's How to Prepare)"** (14 min, 12.5K views day one, TRIAGE **~90% banked / beginner-tier**) — a top-of-funnel explainer for total beginners: *agentic ≠ chat*, "you won't lose your job to AI, you'll lose it to somebody who uses AI" (recycled Jensen-Huang-style line, he only says "the CEO of the biggest tech company"), then 3 Claude Code demos (YouTube-analytics→Excel, a cleaning-business HTML job tracker, 50 Clay-enriched leads + outreach copy) and a 3-step onboarding plan. **Opening stats are unsourced hype** — "agentic AI on its way to replace around 50% of all jobs", "Claude Code being used to do around 60% of the tasks in companies" — do not reuse. Nothing new for us as instruction. **Its value is purely market-signal, and it's a sharp one:** the "learn Claude Code, become an AI manager" pitch is now being mass-marketed to complete non-technical beginners, which is *live evidence* for the commoditisation clock behind [[kunal-shah]]'s trend-vs-business test — the skill is being pushed to everyone right now, so the window to sell it as scarce is closing, exactly as [[src-money-plan-2026-07-22]] concluded. Two smaller notes: his one-skill framing (**"AI manager"** — onboard it, say what good and bad look like, review its output, never accept blindly) is a usable positioning phrase for a non-technical operator who directs agents; and his sample outreach closes with *"I'll run it for free for 30 days, and if it doesn't book you jobs, you owe nothing"* — a **third independent convergence on free-first offers** (with the money-plan T2 free-first sprint and Kunal's work-free-for-access exception). Detail: [[src-nate-herk-replace-millions]].

## Standing lesson

His videos are the best early-warning feed for Claude Code capability shifts — but every study so far ends "we're ahead on infrastructure, behind on audience/monetisation." The gap is never tooling; see [[reach-before-monetisation]]. **2026-07-15: the lead-magnet-machine count across the wiki is now 8** ([[money-is-services-not-adsense]]) — the running read stays "we run most of it already; the video's value is market-signal (what's shifting, what's about to compress), not playbook." **2026-07-23 (7th study) is the purest instance of this pattern yet** — a "5 hacks" video where we already run all 5 at a more mature level; its only value was as the prompt to run his self-audit idea against our own OS, which caught real memory-index drift the video could never have told us about.
