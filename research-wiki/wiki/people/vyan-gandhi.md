# Vyan Gandhi (Augmentables)

22-year-old founder of Augmentables, an Indian AI-video studio. Studied 2026-06-29 via 30-min podcast scrape (Satish K Videos, 2M subs).

## Numbers (as of the interview)

- **₹15L/month revenue, ₹2 crore in 18 months** — ALL from B2B services: brand ads (Mahindra, Vasmol), film production (Mirai), OTT micro-dramas (**Pocket FM ₹60–70K/min**).
- His viral AI Mahabharata trailer (1.8M views) earned **₹0 directly** — it was the portfolio piece that drove a ₹70L Pocket FM deal via inbound calls. The origin case for [[money-is-services-not-adsense]].

## His method

- Stack: Nano-Banana (character consistency) → Midjourney (locations) → Kling + Seedance (animation) → ElevenLabs (audio/dub) — see [[ai-video-stack]].
- 30-min drafts: lock characters → lock locations → first images → client pitch; 3 revision rounds.
- Economics filter: **"AI only profits where it replaces work costing lakhs, done for thousands"** — competing on cheap content is a losing trade.
- Learn-fast: copy reference videos shot-for-shot, kill ideas instantly on first hard problem, YouTube tutorials > paid courses.

## Gotcha we banked

**Hindi dialogue lip-sync in Seedance "looks very fake"; English is clean** — directly relevant to any future Hindi-voice video pipeline. (Our IndicF5 voice-over-with-no-lipsync format sidesteps this.)

Verdict for us (2026-06-29): the service-agency model fails [[passion-fit-gate]]; the economics filter and flagship-demo-reel-beats-daily-treadmill lessons are reusable. Related: [[vaibhav-sisinty]].
