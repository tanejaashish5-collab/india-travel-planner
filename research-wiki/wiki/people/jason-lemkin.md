# Jason Lemkin

SaaStr founder. Studied via 3 of his own AI-agents pieces + his crossover appearance on [[lenny-rachitsky]]'s podcast (the artifact worth reading — his own channel runs short-takes, not deep-reads). Real operator, not a funnel-only creator: runs SaaStr on a live 20-agent fleet.

## The 20-agent fleet, in numbers

1.2 humans orchestrate 20 agents replacing an estimated 8–10 GTM humans. **Orchestration is a headcount line (10–15 hrs/wk), not free** — the single most-corrected assumption from this study (see [[retainer-is-the-business]]). 70% response rate on lapsed-lead reactivation (his own number, n=1, unverified). Mandatory rule once past 5 agents: segment the audience per agent so two agents never contact the same person.

## Enterprise agent economics — the gap a local operator fills

Enterprise agent vendors charge $50–100K/yr plus $25–50K forward-deployed training — a price SMBs structurally cannot buy into. That gap is exactly what a local operator (Corey Ganim's $999-assessment-to-retainer ladder, Brian Casel's install-plus-retainer model) is priced to fill.
