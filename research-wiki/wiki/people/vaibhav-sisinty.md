# Vaibhav Sisinty

Founder of GrowthSchool (ed-tech, claims 10M+ learners / 150+ countries). Ex-Uber/Klook growth. Scale as of 2026-06-30: **740K YouTube subs (646 videos), 2M Instagram, 500K LinkedIn, 150K X**. Studied via full YT+IG scrape (96 IG posts via Instagram's own GraphQL, not thumbnails).

## The machine

- **No secret tool — a funnel**: near-daily AI-news content on 5 platforms → free WhatsApp community (stayingahead.ai, 1M+ members, **per-video tracked join links** `links.stayingahead.com/YTnn`) → paid layers: B2B corporate AI training (clients incl. Adobe, Razorpay, Uber, IIM-A, Publicis Sapient), paid speaking, GrowthSchool free-workshop→paid-cohort funnel. Textbook [[owned-audience-funnel]].
- **Engagement decay measured live**: IG median likes 20.1K (Apr, partial) → 10.9K (May, n=49) → 6.7K (June, n=41) — **−38% May→June median**. Even the category winner is fatiguing on AI-news-jacking → see [[ai-content-saturation]].
- **9% of posts = 38% of likes**: podcast guest-clips (Raj Shamani "Figuring Out"); his best post ever in sample (295K likes) isn't his own production → [[distribution-arbitrage-via-guesting]].
- Growth mechanics: "Comment 'X' and I'll DM you" bait (7/96 posts); every lifestyle post ends with the same WhatsApp CTA; only ONE #ad in sample (Oral-B) — sponsorship incidental, not the model.
- His cinematic-reel stack ("100M views/month" claim): Claude → Nano Banana Pro / GPT Image 2 → Seedance 2.0 / Veo 3.1 → OpenArt VFX. See [[ai-video-stack]].
