# Nitin Joshi (@jinitinjoshi)

Indian business-podcast host. Weekly ~2h Hindi interviews with named Indian operators, released
Wednesdays. **30 videos, 55.1 hours** on the `/videos` tab as of 2026-08-03. Claims 2.5M+
followers. Sells courses via **nitinjoshi.graphy.com**.

**Why he matters to us:** he is the first source we hold that covers **on-ground, real-economy,
capital-deployed Indian businesses** — scrap, tyres, FMCG distribution, EV charging infrastructure,
alcohol, QSR franchising, recommerce. Every prior entry in the ledger is digital/AI/services. That
category carried **zero coverage** until the "on-ground-India-solo = auto-fail" kill-rule was
lifted by founder decision on 2026-08-02 ([[rejected-ideas]]).

## Classification — read with a specific guard

**Not a neutral interview show.** At least some episodes are **franchise lead-generation
placements** where the guest sells a business opportunity to the audience — proven on camera in
the EV-charging episode, where the guest reports 100+ franchise inquiries generated by a prior
podcast appearance. This is the inversion of the guest-operator exception in
[[receipts-over-content]] and is documented there as its own class.

## Credibility — unresolved, flagged not settled

Public bio claims two food ventures built and sold (1,100+ daily orders, acquired ₹2.25 crore)
and ₹6 crore in performance marketing via Futurepreneur Solutions. A Josh Talks episode about him
is titled *"₹70 lakh debt, 2 businesses failed, and then YouTube…"*. These may both be true in
sequence, or the bio may be inflated. **Do not cite his operating record as established.**
