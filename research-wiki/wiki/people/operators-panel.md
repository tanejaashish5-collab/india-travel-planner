# Operators panel (Dan Koe · Hormozi · Isenberg · Marina · Codie + DOAC/Jay guests)

Composite page for the tactical-operator cohort studied 2026-06-19 → 2026-06-24 (10 operator videos ~44K words + 55 deep-read podcast episodes ~1.04M words across DOAC/Jay Shetty).

## The convergent playbook (all of them, independently)

**Productize knowledge → own the asset → sell up a value ladder → validate with money before building → compound patiently.** Ladder: free content → 1:1 high-ticket → cohort → course → community. See [[test-cheap-before-build]] and [[owned-audience-funnel]].

## Per-operator signatures

- **Dan Koe** — productize your 3-interest intersection; one-person-business stack. His "AI learning experience" concept is the shape Ashish has already half-built (Sadhguru-OS/Manifestation-OS/Naval KBs).
- **Alex Hormozi** — value equation (Dream Outcome × Likelihood ÷ Time × Effort); latency beats magnitude; $100M-offers pricing; Pain/Profession/Passion triangulation. **2026-07-23 DOAC update** ([[src-hormozi-doac-reality-moat]], full 142-min read, ~65% banked): the delta = **"reality is the moat"** — in an AI world the only defensible edge is real proof / track record / reputation (only exist in reality). Asked what he'd do facing superintelligence: *"build as much real-world proof and track record as I could."* = 6th independent witness for [[verification-as-moat]] + the money-plan's "build the demo pack" action. Sub-tactics: the "are you making more money?" AI-adoption test (the $350K-to-replace-$11K/mo-VAs cautionary tale), own-the-decision/assume-the-risk (AI is a liability, humans want stakes), proof-of-effort when no proof-of-outcome yet ("record your day, clip the best moment").
- **Greg Isenberg** — presell before build (30-sec prototype, 300 emails, founding-member discount). **2026-07-16 update:** a metadata-only channel triage (this session, no full read) shows his channel has drifted to AI-news/idea-list content — 430 videos, ~300 hrs, not worth a full audit. But guest-operator episodes remain the receipts-bearing exception inside it: a single-video deep-read of his 2026-07-15 episode with Corey Ganim ([[src-ganim-ai-assessment]]) produced more take-able technique in one hour than the last four full channel audits combined (see [[receipts-over-content]]). See [[corey-ganim]] for the guest's own mixed verdict.
- **Marina Mogilko** — 20–30 user interviews before building anything.
- **Codie Sanchez** — buy boring cash-flow businesses (seller financing); 24-hour rule. For us: CONDITIONAL HOLD — off passion lane, operationally heavy from Canberra.
- **DOAC/Jay meta-findings (55 episodes):** vulnerability is a distribution engine; identity follows action; named/numbered frameworks travel; **MindValley proof-case: $75M ARR, zero venture, faceless-ish** — the existence proof for a faceless wisdom-education business at scale.

## Verdicts logged for us (2026-06-24)

- **AI Learning Experience** from existing KBs — PURSUE, standout.
- **1:1 high-ticket wisdom/strategy offer ($500–2K)** — PURSUE, fastest first-revenue path.
- Anti-pattern noted: the loudest DOAC names won via on-camera brand equity, physical inventory, or deep-science moats — all anti-fit for a faceless operator → don't copy their surface, copy the machine ([[money-is-services-not-adsense]]).
