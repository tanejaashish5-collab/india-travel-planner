## The convergent playbook (all of them, independently)

**Productize knowledge → own the asset → sell up a value ladder → validate with money before building → compound patiently.** Ladder: free content → 1:1 high-ticket → cohort → course → community. See [[test-cheap-before-build]] and [[owned-audience-funnel]].
