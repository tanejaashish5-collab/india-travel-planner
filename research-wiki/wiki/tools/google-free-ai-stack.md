# Google free AI stack (mid-2026)

Google's free-tier tools now cover most paid-AI use cases — the most-recycled content theme on [[vaibhav-sisinty]]'s channel (his 8-tool masterclass: 165K views). Inventory as of 2026-07-01:

| Tool | What it does | Relevance to us |
|---|---|---|
| **Gemini** | Chat + Deep Research + Nano Banana images + Omni video | General |
| **Google AI Studio** | Full apps from one prompt; Lyria 3 AI music; AI podcasts | Low (we build in Claude Code) |
| **NotebookLM** | Sources → reports, audio overviews, mind maps, slide decks | We use occasionally; worth more use for founder-readable outputs |
| **Learn Your Way** | Personalized AI tutor | Low |
| **Opal** | No-code AI automations (cold-email machines, catalog bots) | Closest to our `_loop` scripts; look at it before building any new non-technical automation |
| **Stitch** | AI web-app design, exports to AI Studio | Low |
| **Pomelli** | "Business DNA" → ad campaigns, product shoots, brand book, website → pushes into Google Ads | **Highest-relevance**: fast creative/ad assets with no design team, if/when any of our businesses runs paid |
| **Google Flow + Gemini Omni** | AI filmmaking studio + the video model powering it; "Flow TV" exposes other people's prompts | Adjacent to our Veo usage |
| **Antigravity 2.0** | Agent-first dev platform (Claude Code competitor) | Competitive intel only |
| **Gemini Spark** | 24/7 personal agent, runs with laptop closed | Overlaps [[self-hosted-agents]] |

**Pattern note:** "Free Google tool replaces paid X" is itself a saturating content genre — see [[ai-content-saturation]].
