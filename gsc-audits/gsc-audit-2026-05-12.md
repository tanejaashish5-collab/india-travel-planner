## 5. Notable changes & action items

### Wins
- **Performance:** 4.4× clicks, 3× impressions, position +3.0 ranks since Apr 20. The Sprint 13a/13b parity work + GSC sweep are clearly paying off.
- **Indexing:** +6,800 net indexed pages (97% increase). The 404 sweep dropped 289 pages off the broken list.
- **Hindi locale:** First Hindi pages cracking the top-10. The hreflang fix from the memory notes appears to be working.
- **Long-tail capture:** 1,000-query cap is being saturated, indicating the destination/month combinatorial coverage is finding demand.

### Concerns / action items
1. **5,914 "Discovered - currently not indexed"** — by far the biggest indexing blocker. This is the same "discovered-not-indexed" pool that was 11,568 on Apr 20 — it's dropped by ~5,654 (49%), but it's still over half of all non-indexed pages. These are pages Google knows about but has chosen not to crawl/index yet. Typically a crawl-budget / signal-strength issue. Consider: internal linking density to these pages, sitemap freshness, request-indexing on top-priority URLs.
2. **2,670 "Page with redirect"** — much higher than expected. Likely the 307→301 middleware conversion and locale routing — verify these are intentional redirects to canonical pages, not redirect chains.
3. **1,195 "Duplicate without user-selected canonical"** + **161 "Google chose different canonical"** = 1,356 canonical-confusion pages. Worth a spot-check of canonical tag rendering, especially given the next-intl hreflang/x-default history.
4. **751 "Blocked by robots.txt"** — verify this is intentional (likely API routes, /admin paths, etc.) and not accidentally blocking content URLs.
5. **0.4% sitewide CTR is low.** Kasol/may at 783 impressions / 2 clicks (0.26%) is the canary. Title/meta-description audit on top-impression / low-CTR pages should be the next SEO sweep.
6. **404 validation re-run:** The 322→33 win deserves a fresh validation cycle to mark those 289 fixes resolved in GSC and close the alert. Then investigate the remaining 33.

### CTR opportunity (top-impression underperformers worth a snippet pass)
