### 404 Validation update

The 322-page 404 cohort that started validating on Apr 20 has collapsed to **40 pages with Validation: Failed**. That's an 88% reduction. "Failed" status means Google retried these URLs and they still 404 — but the cohort that mattered is now ~40 stragglers, not 322. Either the underlying URLs were fixed, deprecated cleanly via 301, or Google has moved them out of the active retry set.

### Concentration of remaining "not indexed"

- **Discovered–not–indexed** at 6,706 is still the dominant bucket (60% of all not-indexed). Google knows the URLs but hasn't crawled them yet — purely a crawl-budget signal, will resolve as authority grows.
- **Page with redirect (2,605)** + **Duplicate without canonical (1,162)** = 3,767 pages, 34% of not-indexed. These are technical SEO clean-ups: confirm 307→301 conversion is working as intended (the middleware does this), and verify canonical tags on locale-routed pages. Worth a single sweep.
- **Blocked by robots.txt (290)** — sanity check expected (admin, API, etc.). Not a concern unless real content URLs are in there.
