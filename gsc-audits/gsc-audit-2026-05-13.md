## 5. Trajectory & action items

### Big wins since Apr 20
- **Indexed pages nearly doubled** (7K → 13.8K) — sitemap discovery + content quality work is paying off.
- **Discovered-not-indexed dropped from 11,568 to 5,914** (−49%). Google is converting more of the crawl backlog into actual indexing.
- **Total not-indexed shrank** (14.1K → 11.4K) despite the site adding pages — net positive.

### Concerns / action items

1. **404 validation FAILED.** The Apr 20 validation on 322 pages didn't pass — Google now shows 33 pages still 404'ing with the validation marked Failed. Two paths: (a) confirm the remaining 33 URLs really are gone and submit a fresh validation, or (b) restore/redirect them if any are legitimate destination/month combinations that shouldn't be 404. Worth a one-shot triage.
2. **Page with redirect: 2,670** — large bucket. Likely the en/hi locale-prefix migration + 307→301 conversion in middleware. Worth confirming these are 301s (not 307/302) and that the redirect targets are themselves indexable.
3. **Duplicate buckets total 1,501** (1,195 user-canonical + 161 Google-chose-different + 145 alternate-with-canonical). The "Google chose different canonical than user" bucket is the one to inspect — it's where hreflang/canonical signals are being overruled. Cross-check against the hreflang fix memory.
4. **Discovered-not-indexed: 5,914** — still the single largest bucket. Mostly long-tail destination/month combinations Google hasn't gotten to. Internal linking from hub pages (/where-to-go/[month], /region/[id]) to these can accelerate discovery.
5. **CTR at 0.4%** is low against a 9.3 avg position — pages in positions 5–10 should be getting 2–5% CTR. Title/description optimization is the highest-leverage lever here. The Kasol/May page (786 imp, 0.25% CTR) is the canonical example.

### What's working (don't change)
- Hindi pages are punching above their weight in the click table — keep producing them.
- The destination/[month] template is generating impressions across hundreds of unique queries — the URL pattern is the right shape.
- Average position improving 3+ rungs in 3 weeks is real momentum.
