## 5. Key Observations & Action Items

### Wins
- **Indexed +680 in 6 days** (7,000 → 7,680). Google is steadily working through the discovered queue.
- **Discovered-not-indexed −651** (11,568 → 10,917). The bottleneck is shrinking, mirroring the indexed gain. This is the right direction.
- **Position 12.7 → 11.9** in a week. The Sprint 8 thin-content fix and Sprint 3 depth backfill are translating into ranking gains, not just indexing.
- **Impressions nearly tripled WoW** (7,940 → 22,700). Visibility is compounding fast.

### Concerns / Action Items

1. **404 validation still "Started" after 6 days, page count grew (322 → 335).** The +13 net new 404s suggest Google is still discovering broken URLs faster than the validation can clear. Worth checking if the validation has actually attempted any URLs yet, or if there's a fresh source of 404s (sitemap drift, stale internal links). If the original 322 still haven't been re-fetched, consider hitting "Validate fix" again on the cohort.

2. **Non-localised URL leak on /destination/kumbhalgarh/may.** Top-10 impression page is served without `/en/` prefix. Likely a middleware edge case or a stale link somewhere. Check `apps/web/src/middleware.ts` and grep the codebase for absolute `/destination/` paths missing the locale.

3. **CTR dipped 0.3 → 0.2.** Not alarming on its own (impressions grew 86% DoW), but the top-impression queries (yercaud weather in may with 114, pondicherry weather in may with 112) are 0-click. These are pages ranking on page 2 — push Sprint 9 NakshIQ-100/Cost-Index citations to lift the ranking ones at the borderline of page 1.

4. **Discovered-not-indexed at 10,917 is still the dominant bottleneck.** Even at the current burn rate (~100/day getting indexed), this would take ~100 days to clear. Worth considering: (a) submitting more URL sets via IndexNow if not already, (b) checking which page templates dominate the discovered bucket — internal-linking gaps can starve those URLs of crawl signal.

5. **GSC data has a 1–3 day lag.** Today's "performance" totals reflect Apr 16–22; today's "indexing" reflects Apr 20. Will keep monitoring as the lag catches up.
