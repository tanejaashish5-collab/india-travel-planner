**404 bucket (265 pages) — unchanged count and validation state from 09-18** (Not Started, first detected 4/18/26). New this run: **opened the drilldown and sampled the 20 most-recently-crawled example URLs.** All 20, with no exceptions, were Next.js build asset chunks — `https://www.nakshiq.com/_next/static/chunks/<hash>.js` or `.css` with a `?dpl=dpl_<deployment-id>` query string — last crawled between Sep 10–15, 2026. None were content/destination pages, and none matched the previously-documented `festivals/*` slug-collision class from the 08-10 fix. This looks like ordinary Vercel/Next.js deploy churn: old HTML (cached by Google or a visitor) references JS/CSS chunk filenames from a prior build, and those filenames 404 once a new deployment rotates the hashes out. That's a plausible, largely benign explanation for why this bucket grew 221→265 between 09-16 and 09-18 (more deploys → more stale chunk references accumulating faster than Google re-crawls and drops them). **Caveat: this is a sample of 20 of 265, biased toward the most-recent end of the list** (sorted by last-crawled descending), so it doesn't rule out older content-page 404s sitting further back in the bucket — a full export would be needed to characterize the whole 265 with confidence.

**vs Apr 20 baseline:** indexed +184% (7,000 → 19,900), not-indexed +44% (14,100 → 20,300); indexed share of total is 49.5% today vs 33.2% on Apr 20.
