### Key indexing movements vs Apr 20 baseline

- **Discovered – currently not indexed: 11,568 → 3,814 (−7,754).** Massive drop. The backlog of pages Google knew about but never crawled is two-thirds smaller. This is the lever that moved indexed pages from 7K to 15.9K.
- **Not found (404) validation: FAILED.** The Apr 20 validation that started for 322 pages has completed and *failed*. Only 27 pages remain in this bucket, so the failure is mostly cosmetic — the bulk got cleaned up — but the failure means GSC will not auto-retry. Need to manually re-trigger validation on the 27 stragglers once any underlying redirects/410s are in place.
- **Page with redirect (2,705):** Large but expected — this is locale routing + month-rollover URLs. Not actionable unless we see it growing.
- **Duplicate without user-selected canonical (1,183):** Worth a sample-URL look — this is likely the `/destination/...` vs `/en/destination/...` consolidation still in flight.
- **Server error (5xx): 1.** Single page; investigate if it persists.
- **Soft 404: 3.** Still down to single digits, no regression from prior runs.
