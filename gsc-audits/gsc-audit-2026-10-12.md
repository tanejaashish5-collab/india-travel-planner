**The whole net change in this snapshot traces to one bucket: "Crawled – currently not indexed" jumped +1,143 pages (+9.8%), almost exactly matching the -1.1K drop in indexed pages.** Every other reason moved by double digits at most. This reads as pages that were indexed getting re-crawled and placed into Google's "crawled but not indexed" holding bucket rather than being re-indexed — the most direct technical explanation available for why clicks and impressions have been sliding for four straight reads (§1): fewer indexed pages generally means fewer pages eligible to show in search results.
