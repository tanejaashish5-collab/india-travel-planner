## 5. Trajectory & action items

### Wins since 5/13
- **Indexed pages crossed 14K** (13.8K → 14.8K, +7% in 2 days). Discovery is still converting.
- **Discovered-not-indexed dropped another 921** (5,914 → 4,993, −16%) — the most-improved bucket, second straight audit with double-digit % decline. Internal-linking pressure or sitemap improvements are landing.
- **Crawled-not-indexed dropped 15%** (500 → 426) — these were already-crawled URLs Google wasn't sure about; the shrinkage suggests quality signals are firming up.
- **404 bucket shrank 33 → 26** even though the validation is still marked Failed — Google has organically cleared 7 of them. The remaining 26 is small enough to triage manually.

### Concerns
- **Clicks/impressions tilted down vs 5/13** (−10% / −6%). Position and CTR are flat, so the dip is volume-of-traffic-eligible rather than ranking degradation. May–11 Sunday weekend effect is the most likely explanation — worth confirming on 5/16 whether the curve recovers.
- **404 validation still Failed.** 26 URLs remaining (down from 33). Action: either submit a fresh validation now that the bucket is smaller, or pull the URL list and decide per-URL whether to restore/redirect. The Apr 20 validation has now been failed for ~4 weeks.
- **Page with redirect: 2,679** — unchanged from 5/13 (+9). The locale-prefix migration redirect bucket is not draining. Worth one targeted check: are these 301s with indexable targets, or 307s still in flight?
- **Duplicate-Google-chose-different bucket grew** (161 → 172, +11). Small absolute number but the only canonical-related bucket growing — hreflang regression worth checking against the next-intl/canonical memory.

### What's working (don't change)
- Hindi (/hi/) pages continue to over-perform on CTR.
- Discovered-not-indexed is draining ~900/week — keep the internal-linking / hub-page strategy.
- Indexed-pages count is on a steady upward slope (7K → 13.8K → 14.8K over 25 days).

### Priority action queue (carrying over from 5/13)
1. **Re-validate the 26 remaining 404s** — bucket is small enough now that a manual list-pull + sitemap check + fresh validation submit is a one-sitting task.
2. **Title/meta optimisation on Kasol/May and Munnar/June** — both still at sub-1% CTR with 500+ impressions each. Unchanged from 5/13; this is the single highest-leverage CTR lever on the site.
3. **Audit "Page with redirect" 2,679 bucket** — confirm 301 vs 307, confirm targets are indexable.
