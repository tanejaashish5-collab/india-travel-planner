## 5. Concerns / action items

1. **Indexed pages down ~1K, Crawled–currently-not-indexed up ~1.1K to 13.9K.** Google is dropping/declining templated pages. Sample URL Inspection on pages that recently left the index; prioritise consolidating or enriching thin templated pages (destination/[id]/[month], cost, vs).
2. Traffic is flat (547 clicks) despite the index shrink so far — watch the next 2–3 reads; if impressions keep sliding while the index shrinks, clicks will follow.
3. 404 validation stuck on Failed (305): fix, 301, or 410 these URLs, drop from sitemap, then restart validation.
4. Redirect error rose to 8 and flipped to Failed — inspect those URLs.
5. Redirect (5.2K), robots-blocked (1.5K), noindex (1.3K) buckets are stable; confirm intentional.
6. Seasonal pages (Tungnath October, Kedarnath late-October) are winning — publish similar timely content (Nov/Dec winter destinations) ahead of season.
7. "morni hills weather" CTR still low — rewrite title/meta.
8. Open page 2 of the "Why pages aren't indexed" table next run to see the 2 hidden reasons.

## 6. Follow-up (same day): can it be "fixed"?

URL Inspection sample (n=70, seed 11, sitemap N=25,060): **58.6% indexed (95% CI 46.9–69.4%) ≈ 14.7K**, consistent with the dashboard's 16.2K. Of the not-indexed sample, 23 of 29 were "Crawled – currently not indexed", 4 "Discovered – currently not indexed", 1 duplicate-without-canonical, 1 redirect. A first 150-URL run was cut off by the shell time limit, so per-template rates are not reliable at this sample size.

**Conclusion:** no site bug found. The drop is Google declining templated pages after crawling them (a quality/duplication judgement), so it cannot be fixed by a config or code change; the 404 (305), redirect-error (8), robots/noindex buckets are previously triaged as intentional or decay (see non-indexing-audit-2026-05-06, gsc-failures-triage-2026-06-12). Nothing was changed in the repo and nothing was pushed or deployed.

**To actually move the number (needs founder decision):** pick the thinnest templates (largest not-indexed families), then either deepen content, consolidate, or noindex + drop from sitemap. That needs a per-family not-indexed breakdown — export the "Crawled – currently not indexed" drilldown from GSC (or run the sweep with a larger sample in chunks) before choosing.
