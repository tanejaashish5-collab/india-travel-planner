### Interpreting the +197 movement (with context from non-indexing-audit)

The non-indexing audit (`non-indexing-audit-2026-05-06.md`) verified each of these buckets against live URLs and concluded most are **decay queues, snapshot lag, or working as intended**. Applying that lens to today's deltas:

- **Robots.txt +178 (562 → 740): not a regression.** The 5/06 deep-dive verified all 561 sampled blocked URLs were `/_next/static/chunks/*` and `/_next/static/media/*` — bundler output, correctly disallowed. The +178 jump is most likely additional `_next` chunk URLs entering Google's known-set as new builds shipped (each Vercel deploy generates new chunk hashes, all blocked by robots). **This bucket can safely grow without alarm.** If you want to confirm, sample 5 of the new URLs — but don't budget engineering time on the assumption it's a regression.
- **Page with redirect +24:** stale-Google decay from `/destination/X/Y` → `/en/destination/X/Y` 301s. Will keep drifting up for 3-6 months as Google re-crawls old URLs. No fix possible.
- **Crawled - not indexed +31 (44 → 75):** the one bucket worth looking at. The 5/06 deep-dive found ~30 dest×month pages + 3 thin dest-roots (surat, namchi, kumbalangi) in this bucket. The +31 jump could be more thin pages getting decided-against by Google. **Action: the I1 task in the non-indexing audit (investigate the 3 thin dest-roots) is now more pressing.**
- **Discovered – not indexed -59:** continuing to drain, healthy direction.
- **Duplicate without canonical 1,200, flat:** the 5/03 hreflang/canonical fix (commit 3ab4d8ef) is deployed; this bucket will only update on Google's next re-crawl of long-tail pages (1-4 weeks). Don't expect movement until ~2026-05-31.
